# Lesson 22: Forbidding Actions: Please Don't Take Photos (Learner)

This chapter was easy to study and felt well built. It does one thing, "please don't do X" (ないでください), and it does it carefully. Each verb I already knew gets a casual ない-form card with a "casual form" hint and a note giving the polite equivalent and the sound change (かう→かわない, まつ→またない, くる→こない). The two traps are called out: かえる is Regular 1 so it gives かえらない, and すう (smoke) gives すわない, not the すわらない of すわります (sit). The extras then use each ない-form in a short sentence built almost entirely from words I knew well: としょかん, ゆびわ, むすめ, じてんしゃ, ちゅうしゃじょう, てがみ. Where a sentence brings in a new word (こおり, まだ, わすれます, しんぱい, からく), a one-line note teaches it. My problems were a hint that gives away its own answer, one sentence that needs a word nobody taught me, two pairs of "don't" fronts I could not tell apart, and a few notes written for the builders rather than for me.

## Strong spots

- The ない-form set (`nai-kawanai` through `nai-kikanai`) is consistent: the same "casual form" hint on every front, the same note shape, and each note names the sound change. `nai-kaeranai` and `nai-suwanai` warn about the two verbs that would trip me up.
- `tabako-wo-suimasu` warns about すいます vs すわります before I meet `nai-suwanai`, and `suu-dict-form` closes the loop.
- The sign words hang together: `tachiirikinshi` and `chuushakinshi` share きんし and say so, and `kinen-no-smoking` explains why it does not. The extras then use each sign in a sentence (`no-entry-request`, `no-parking-sign`, `no-smoking-forbid`).
- New words inside sentences get a note: `travel-no-ice` (こおり), `travel-dont-go-yet` (まだ), `travel-dont-forget` (わすれます), `travel-not-spicy` (からく, which also admits it has not been taught yet).
- The にも cards (`also-in-fridge`, `onsen-also-in-hakone`, `also-in-meeting-room`) are easy because the base card `nimo-also-at` says plainly that the に stays and も is added.
- `convenience-store-nearby-a`, `restaurant-no-ketchup` and `no-entry-reply` carry scenes that tell me exactly what is being answered.
- `wasabi-wo-ireru-mae-ni-tabenaide` and the `-dict-form-ex1` cards tie this lesson to the まえに pattern from Lesson 17.

## Weak spots

**leaks-answer**

- `mae-ago` (Recognition): the hint that tells this まえ apart from "before" and "in front of" says "as in 300 years ago", which is the answer. The same hint starts with "after", which points the wrong way on a まえ card (filed as `other`).

**untaught-prerequisite**

- `ireru-box-pen`: needs はこ (box), which no card has taught. It has no note, unlike the こおり and まだ cards next to it.

**ambiguous**

- `travel-dont-worry`: "Please don't worry." is しんぱいしないでください, but `ki-ni-suru-no-wa-yokunai` ends "...so please don't worry." with きにしないでください, and `ki-ni-shinaide-kudasai` is "Please don't worry about it." Nothing on the Production front tells me which verb is wanted.
- `please-dont-photo` vs `toranaide-kudasai`: "Please don't take photos." and "Please don't take [photos]." differ only by brackets (polish).

**other**

- `osake-ex1`: "sake" for おさけ, where the base note says おさけ is alcohol in general and a sister card says "alcohol".
- `iremasu-put-in`: note cites "Marked (R2) in the book" and "a ru-verb", neither of which I have.
- Unit, chapter-22: verb groups are written "Regular I/II" on some notes and "Regular 1/2" on others. Lesson 15 taught 1 and 2.
- Unit, chapter-22-extras: three notes are builder provenance or jargon (`sawarimasu-touch-ex1`, `nai-nomanai-ex1`, `nai-kawanai-ex1`).
