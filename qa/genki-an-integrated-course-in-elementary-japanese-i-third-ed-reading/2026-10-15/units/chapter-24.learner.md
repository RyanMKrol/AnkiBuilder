# Chapter 25 (Reading and Writing 11: Looking for Friends/Members): Learner

This chapter was easy to study, and it felt like a payoff. Most of its kanji were ones I already had as single cards from the Lessons: 好, 近, 明, 病, 映, 画, 旅, 有, 強, 勉, 所, 館, 紙, 歌, 文, 発, 急 and 計. So most of the 44 word cards could be worked out from their parts before I turned them over: 映画館, 旅館, 最近, 近所, 台所, 急病, 発明, 計画, 和紙, 手話. The twelve new single kanji (募, 力, 台, 役, 表, 説, 市, 盗, 集, 平, 重, 詞) each come back in a word in the same unit, so none of them sits alone. Every word card has its romaji. None of the English collides with a word I learned earlier, and the one word that comes close (市長 next to 町長) is told apart by its English. I found nothing worth a finding.

## Strong spots

- New kanji are used straight away in words: 市 (r-7c130d80b872) in 市長 (r-b847fad44608), 市場 (r-b26ab813edca) and 市役所 (r-fca47080a97d), which also uses 役 (r-c3e4e0f1ed02); 表 (r-6a954390e365) in 表紙 (r-787d9ce94632); 説 (r-effdb4ba2286) in 説明 (r-f6c2c8d0f462); 募 (r-1bd6856faf78) and 集 (r-2e5f0bf2c0a7) together in 募集 (r-302a420e86ca); 盗 (r-00b6cd9d86ef) in 強盗 (r-c7f7a9b8af7b); 平 (r-3c9abaf959cc) in 平日 (r-e0400e9328ef); 重 (r-3be8888544d5) in 重病 (r-86cedb580d42); 詞 (r-77a0c88d4eac) in 歌詞 (r-2ea817c7720e); 台 (r-dda4f85fa0dc) in 台所 (r-cfa298cca1c7); 力 (r-a81c2ab80e37) in 強力な (r-e5752ab6e0ad).
- Words that build on earlier ones: 映画館 (r-70ee6ceb8dfd) on 映画 and 館; 漫画 (r-6a0b30d36103) on 漫画家 from Lesson 11; 画家 (r-1d695378fc6d) next to 作家 and 漫画家; お返事 (r-fb865ee8d381) on 返事 from Reading and Writing 8, with "(polite)" in the English to separate the two.
- 市長 "Mayor" (r-b847fad44608) and 町長 "Mayor of a town" from Reading and Writing 10 are a good pair. The English tells them apart, and the kanji shows why.
- The 強 words (強い, 強力な, 強情な, 強盗) show its readings tsuyo, kyō and gō side by side. 強情な and 強盗 agree on gō, so the less obvious reading is taught twice.

## Weak spots

None. I checked a few cards closely and they hold up:

- 好きな "To like" (r-0e4843f20639) and 大好きな "To love" (r-d8ef02dcebae) repeat Lesson 5's 好き and 大好き with な added. Their English matches what I learned, so I answer them correctly.
- 有る "To exist" (r-156efeaf0de7) has the same English as the single kanji 有. The fronts differ, so I am never unsure which card I am on.
- 有能な (r-4479972b323b) uses 能, which I only knew as "Noh". The back teaches the word as a whole, so this is new learning, not a missing prerequisite.
