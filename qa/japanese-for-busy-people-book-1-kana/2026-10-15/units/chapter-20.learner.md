# Lesson 20: Going Places (3): How Do You Go There? (Learner)

This chapter was mostly easy to study. It has two halves. The first is a set of travel verbs (でます, のります, おります, つきます, かかります, plus います for a stay), each with a particle card for the を or に it takes. The second is a long run of duration words: minutes, hours, days, weeks, months and years. The duration cards are repetitive, but every awkward sound change (いっしゅうかん, ろっかげつ, よじかん, よねん) has a short note saying what changes. The day cards carry a "length of time" hint, so they never clash with the Lesson 7 dates I already knew (よっか the 4th, むいか the 6th). The extras sentences reuse words I knew well (ちかてつ, しんかんせん, くうこう, ふたつめ) and only use the new verbs and counters, so almost every sentence was answerable. Where a short answer could have meant several things, it had a scene. My problems came down to two fronts I could not pin down, one note I did not trust, and one sentence that needed a word I had only learned as "fire".

## Strong spots

- The particle pair `wo-departure-particle` and `ni-particle-vehicle-arrival` names the verbs on the front, so they never collide with the object を or the destination に. `train-off-bus-on` then puts both in one sentence, and its note says which is which.
- The day-duration cards (`yokka`, `muika`, `itsuka`, `mikka`, `futsuka`) are hinted against the dates and tie back to Lesson 7 in the note. `ichinichi` warns off ついたち.
- `ichijikan` sets ぐらい against ごろ, which matches the Lesson 13 ごろ card.
- Short answers all carry a scene: `how-long-hongkong-a`, `stay-one-week`, `stayed-four-days`, `leave-at-730`, `arrive-at-8`, `shinkansen-about-4-hours`. `paul-want-to-go-tokyo-dome` uses its scene to tell itself apart from いきたいです.
- `ichinen-han` and `kan-period-suffix` agree on the one exception (no かん before はん).

## Weak spots

**Ambiguous**

- `ikitai-n-desu-ga` (Production): "I'd like to go" is what Lesson 17's いきたいです answers. No hint asks for the request-opener form. (should-fix)

**Unanswerable**

- `ka-nichi` (Production): "…days (counter), counting days" fits にち as well as か, and the card's own note says both are used. (should-fix)

**Note unverifiable**

- `kakarimasu-2`: "Used when the speaker feels the time is long" does not match the chapter's own かかります sentences for 15 and 30 minutes. (should-fix)

**Untaught prerequisite**

- `yasumi-ni-nebou-suru-no-ga-suki` (Production): やすみのひ needs ひ as "day". The only ひ I learned means fire, so I would say やすみは. (should-fix)

**Missing info**

- `yasumi-ni-nebou-suru-no-ga-suki` (Production): "sleeping in" is not the gloss I was given for ねぼう, so ねる fits the front just as well. (polish)

**Other**

- `yokka` (also `futsuka`, `muika`, `itsuka`, `mikka`): the Production front prints "a length of time" twice. (polish)
- `neboushimasu`, `nebou-shimashita`: the romaji is nebōshimasu here and nebō shimasu on every other ねぼう card. (polish)
- `kan-period-suffix`: the note mentions "the chart", which I have never seen. (polish)
- `noru-dict` and the other nine dictionary and て-form cards: the note is only "R1 verb" or "R2 verb", an abbreviation no earlier card used. (polish)
