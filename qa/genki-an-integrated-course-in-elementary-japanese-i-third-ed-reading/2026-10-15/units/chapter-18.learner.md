# Chapter 19: Reading and Writing 5: Travel (chapter-18), Learner

A short, friendly chapter of 36 cards. Most of it falls into two sets: the days of the month (一日 to
十日, plus 二十日) and a family of 男 / 女 / 天 / 気 / 見 / 食 words. The single kanji 天, 気, 見,
食, 男 and 女 had already been taught in earlier lessons, so nearly every compound read from parts I
held, and the four new characters (性, 才, 私, 田) each come back straight away in a compound of this
unit. Nothing on any card is wrong as far as the deck has taught me. My three findings are all
polish, and they all come down to how one gloss sits next to a card I learned earlier.

## Strong spots

- **The dates read from the number cards.** The kun readings on the Lesson 4 number kanji (六 mui,
  七 nano, 八 yō, 九 kokono, 十 tō, 五 itsu, 四 yot-, 二 futa) plus 日's ka give 六日 muika
  (r-c2a9c36ead2b), 七日 nanoka (r-2fa75ea7bb73), 八日 yōka (r-e36f6e40c271), 九日 kokonoka
  (r-8f97fb7a6fdd), 十日 tōka (r-9d42d77d2761), 五日 itsuka (r-bfd90f8265e5), 四日 yokka
  (r-cbabb6f53e1c) and 二日 futsuka (r-74cb49a695f9). Only 一日 tsuitachi (r-d193f69c03f5) and 二十日
  hatsuka (r-cc0e264cde77) are irregular, and those two backs teach them.
- **Formal and plain pairs are marked.** 朝食 "Breakfast (formal)" (r-c636cb0ec37e) sits apart from
  the earlier 朝ご飯 "Breakfast". 男性 / 女性 "Man / Woman (formal)" (r-c66e98ce0735,
  r-98ef09539013) sit apart from 男の人 and 女の人. No back left me unsure which word I had.
- **New characters come back at once.** 田 (r-2380e6711d81) gives ta, which 田んぼ (r-0941526f94fe)
  uses. 性 (r-6dc93688a090) gives sei, used in 男性 and 女性. 天 (heaven) carries through 天皇
  (r-3661936c6a0e), 天国 (r-e7df6ab36b66) and 天才 (r-aae0a78fb060).
- **Compounds from known parts.** 花見 (r-54fcfd2d8342), 見物 (r-54c6b07d9925), 食事
  (r-f0ddd1ecf81f), 人気 (r-8c824404672f), 気持ち (r-cdf4fcfd03ee) and 一行目 (r-88219c1f683d) all
  read from earlier single-kanji cards. では、お元気で (r-31825d8efb50) builds on 元気.

## Weak spots

**missing-info**

- 一日 (r-d193f69c03f5): the previous unit taught 三日 as "Three days" and 二日間 as "Two days", so
  my first guess on 一日 was "one day". The back says tsuitachi, the 1st, and nothing tells me that
  "one day" is the same characters with a different reading (ichinichi). I would come away thinking
  一日 is always tsuitachi.

**other**

- Unit-level: the date cards gloss only "The Nth (day of the month)", while 三日 from the last unit
  was "Three days" with the same kind of reading. When I answered 四日 as "four days" the back did
  not tell me whether I was wrong.
- 才 (r-b27790d8cdf7): "Genius; years old" collides in meaning with 天才 "Genius" in this unit, so I
  cannot see what 天 adds. Nothing connects it to 歳 (sai, "Age; years old"), which I learned last
  unit with the same sound and the same use.

Two things I noticed and did not count as findings. 私 (r-4bbe59fd177f) only gives watashi, so the
shi in 私立大学 (r-e6a89109e8de) is new to me, but that card teaches it on its back. 性 and 田 are
silent while 才 and 私 have audio, but preflight passes that split and the deck has done the same
with 歳 before.
