# Chapter 11: Lesson 9: Kabuki (chapter-10), Learner

This chapter studied well. It's heavy on kanji: 41 single-kanji cards, then 52 words, and most of
the words are built from kanji taught here or earlier, so a front I had never seen could usually be
read from its parts. Three sets make the chapter: colours (the kanji, the い adjectives and the 色
compounds), the native counters 一つ to 九つ, and the traditional arts (the 道 words, plus 歌舞伎,
能, 狂言, 文楽 and 落語). Each set reinforces itself on the shuffle. Only three cards gave me an
answer I could not have reached from what I had been taught, and each is a polish-level gap on the
back, not a broken card.

## Strong spots

- **The colour family reads from its parts.** 色 (r-1391f4a4c627) gives iro. After that, 水色
  (r-d2dddf0d4414), 紺色 (r-ed644386b2e6), 灰色 (r-5996c71fae9f), 金色 (r-c2cd3188883f), 銀色
  (r-a2d1c9501874) and 茶色い (r-d0dc22224c0b) all read from kanji I hold. 黄色い
  (r-729ea6ac0458) builds from 黄's ki. 白黒 (r-fbd943278ab9) reads from the kun readings of
  both kanji.
- **The counters are fully supported.** Every one of 一つ to 九つ (r-d28d96c108f3, r-5fcadade2558,
  r-7171cf423e66, r-56a0698cb900, r-c664873dbc35, r-812529a34f6c, r-0f00cd9cdf63, r-8d2549b3b312,
  r-12b00051b9b1) matches a kun reading on the number-kanji cards from Lesson 4 (hito, futa, mit-,
  yot-, itsu, mut-, nana, yat-, kokono).
- **始まる / 始める.** 始まる (r-9e8802daf9aa) is glossed '(Something) begins', which separates it
  from Lesson 8's 始める 'To begin (something)'.
- **The 道 arts** (茶道 r-60e42c1a2a9b, 書道 r-8c23d9f9cd4b, 剣道 r-07e1aa84c08b, 華道
  r-ae5d54c4c826, 柔道 r-1514db944029) all read from their kanji. Even sadō works, because the
  Lesson 3 茶 card lists sa.
- **Opaque-looking words are taught on the back:** 意地悪 (r-4d585a3497f0), 是非
  (r-8136f8d8bcd9) and 試験 (r-a86d6df82827) all read cleanly from kanji taught in this unit.

## Weak spots

**missing-info (3, all polish)**

- 相撲 (r-2b0bc6a7f5c8): sumō is a whole-word reading. This unit's 相 (sō, shō / ai) and 撲 (boku)
  lead to sōboku, and the back doesn't say the reading is special. This is the same gap earlier
  chapters had with 今日 and 今年.
- 歌舞伎 (r-ce25b07bd53a): the only 歌 card (Lesson 7) gives just uta, so the ka of kabuki looks
  arbitrary. Adding ka to the 歌 card fixes it.
- 青信号 (r-244232298a54): every 青 card says 'Blue', so 'Green light' marks me wrong, and nothing
  says Japanese calls this green 青.

Preflight reported nothing for this unit apart from the two excluded cards, which are not shown.
