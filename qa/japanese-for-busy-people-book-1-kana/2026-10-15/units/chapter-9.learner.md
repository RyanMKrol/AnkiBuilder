# Lesson 9 (chapter-9 + chapter-9-extras): learner pass

This chapter mostly went well. It brings in the target に (meet, tell, send, telephone, ask), the frequency words from いつも down to ぜんぜん, the own-family vs. someone-else's-family pairs, and a run of restaurant phrases. Nearly every sentence is built from words I already had or from this chapter's word cards, and the question/answer pairs have scene hints that tell me which question a short reply answers. The minimal pairs in the extras (かいしゃに vs かいしゃで, ともだちに vs ともだちと) are the best teaching in the chapter. The problems come in four groups. The spouse notes say つま/かない and おっと/しゅじん are "readings of the same word", which the deck's own かない and しゅじん cards contradict. A few Production fronts don't say which of two forms I know they want (お or not, ください or おねがいします, は or not, the short にします). Two everyday verbs, おきます and ねます, turn up in sentences with no word card. And a set of hints names the very word the front is testing.

## Strong spots

- **Target に contrasts.** p9-kaisha-ni-meru-o-okurimasu / p9-kaisha-de-meru-o-okurimasu and p9-tomodachi-ni-denwa-o-shimasu / p9-tomodachi-to-denwa-o-shimasu are clean minimal pairs, and each note explains the other half. aimasu-meet's note ruling out と is the right warning at the right moment.
- **ききます, two meanings.** kikimasu-ask's hint ("not listening to audio") and note keep it apart from Lesson 8's ききます; ask-phone-number / tell-phone-number pair it against おしえます.
- **Family vocabulary.** chichi/otousan, haha/okaasan, okusan, goshujin, and the extras' x9f- sets (musuko/musukosan, ani/oniisan, sofu/ojiisan, kyoudai/gokyoudai, ryoushin/goryoushin) all have "(My)" vs "(Another person's)" on the front, so no pair collides. tsuma/kanai and otto/shujin are told apart by "modern" vs "traditional" hints.
- **Frequency scale.** yoku-often's note ranks all five words, and the f9- library drills (f9-yoku-, f9-tokidoki-, f9-amari-, f9-zenzen-toshokan-ni-ikimasu) let me practise the scale on one frame.
- **Short replies with a scene.** yoku-hon-a, yoku-library-a, p9-suzuki-san-ni-aimasu, ex9-nakamura-san-ni-okurimasu, fib-uchi-de-tabemasu. ex9-ginko-ni-shimasu's hint explaining the dropped でんわを is exactly what a learner needs.
- **Restaurant register.** suzuki-sama, ex9-suzuki-sama-desu-ne, ex9-kochira-desu and ex9-onomimono-wa (tied back to Lesson 8's ささきさんは) each say who is speaking and why.
- **Counters.** ex9-nama-biru-o-futatsu's note on つ for draft beer vs ほん for bottles is a useful thing to know.

## Weak spots

**wrong-content**

- chapter-9/meet-monday-4 (should-fix): the note says the sentence stacks three に; it has two.
- chapter-9/tsuma-wife, chapter-9/otto-husband (should-fix): "Also read かない / しゅじん" presents two different words as one word read two ways. The kanai-wife ("literally inside the house") and shujin-husband ("literally master") cards say otherwise.
- chapter-9-extras/v9-kanai-wa-kaimono, v9-tsuma-to-kabuki, v9-shujin-wa-nyusu, v9-otto-wa-jimu (should-fix): the same "two readings of the same word" claim in each sentence note.

**ambiguous / unanswerable**

- chapter-9/onomimono vs chapter-9-extras/drink (should-fix): "Beverage" and "Drink / the noun" want おのみもの and のみもの with nothing to choose between them.
- chapter-9/beer-order, chapter-9-extras/ex9-nama-biru-o-futatsu, ex9-sore-o-futatsu-onegaishimasu (polish): "Two X, please." wants おねがいします here, while v9-gurasu-o-futatsu's "Two wine glasses, please." and the Lesson 5 beer cards want ください.

**missing-info**

- chapter-9/dare-ni-denwa-a (should-fix): no cue for the short なかむらさんにします, unlike its sibling ex9-ginko-ni-shimasu.
- chapter-9-extras/ex9-senshu-wa-koko-de-aimashita (should-fix): nothing asks for the は after せんしゅう, though the other two contrastive-は cards say "As for ...".
- chapter-9-extras/p102-nihongo-o-benkyou-shimasu (should-fix): べんきょうをします loses its を with no note.

**untaught-prerequisite**

- chapter-9-extras/p102-mainichi-nanji-ni-okimasu-ka (should-fix): おきます has no word card (also in p102-hachiji-ni-okimasu).
- chapter-9-extras/p102-sakuban-nanji-ni-nemashita-ka (should-fix): ねます has no word card (also in p102-juuniji-ni-nemashita), and the note explains さくばん with きのうのよる, using an untaught よる.

**leaks-answer**

- chapter-9/ya-suffix, go-language-suffix, ni-particle-target (polish): the Recognition hint states the meaning being asked for.
- chapter-9-extras/v9-kanai-wa-kaimono, v9-tsuma-to-kabuki, v9-shujin-wa-nyusu, v9-otto-wa-jimu, v9-gurasu-o-futatsu (polish): the Production hint prints the word whose choice is the point of the card.

**other**

- chapter-9/kikimasu-ask (polish): scene and hint print as two near-identical lines on the Production front.
- chapter-9 unit (polish): notes point by position ("above", "below", "the previous card").
- chapter-9-extras unit (polish): five hints or notes cite the book's dialogue, notes or Exercise VII.
- chapter-9-extras/f9-nihon-wa-do-desu-ka, x9f-kodomo (polish): notes point forward to Lessons 11 and 13.
