## Findings

**book-gap**

- `-` (様 / 〜さま), should-fix: the postcard glosses 〜さま "Mr./Ms. (used in letter writing)" and
  the addresses note glosses 様 "Mr./Ms.". The coverage pass listed 様 and said it would merge さま
  into the kanji form, but the merge kept the kana さま, which went to the kana pool and was not
  chosen. No card in the collection has either form.
- chapter-17 `r-482dccb7367c` (三日), should-fix: this chapter teaches 三日 (みっか) in the series of
  dates, the 3rd of the month. The build dropped it as already carded, and the RW4 card says only
  "Three days", so 二日 reads "The 2nd (day of the month)" while 三日 never does.

**other**

- `-` (今, 山), polish: the table teaches 今(いま) "now" and 山(やま) "mountain" as words with one
  reading each, but both ship only as silent character cards from earlier units (今 "Now", kon, kin /
  ima; 山 "Mountain", san / yama). 私 and 川, taught the same way, are voiced word cards.
- `r-31825d8efb50` (では、お元気で), polish: "Take care." keeps the book's full stop. 28 of the
  collection's 30 greeting cards have none.
