# Lesson 10: Describing Things: It's Delicious (learner)

This was a comfortable chapter to study. It is almost all adjectives, and it builds them up in a sensible way: first the bare word, then a short sentence using it, then a question and answer pair with a scene saying what the answer replies to, then the negative. The い/な split comes up on the back of nearly every card that needs it, and the negatives (あつくない, おおきくない, しずかじゃありません) each come with the rule written out in the note. The one irregular form, よくない, is explained twice. Most of my trouble came from notes: one gives a false grammar label, and two point to cards by titles I could not find. Two Production fronts also want an answer that a card I already know gives to the same English.

## Strong spots

- The "cool" pair is kept apart by its hints: すずしい gets "pleasantly cool weather" and かっこいい gets "stylish, good-looking", with a note saying which is which (chapter-10/suzushii, chapter-10-extras/cool).
- The second いただきます has a scene on its Recognition front ("accepting something you have just been offered") and a note setting it against the mealtime one from chapter 0, so I never mix them up (chapter-10/itadakimasu-accept).
- The trap with きれい is spelled out well: ending in い, but a な-adjective, so there is no きれいくない (chapter-10-extras/f10-koppu-kirei-ja-arimasen, kore-wa-kirei-na-kaban-desu, pretty).
- Each question and answer pair carries a scene saying what is being answered, so a bare はい、きれいです or いいえ、さむくないです can be studied on its own (chapter-10/tokyo-atsukunai-a, chapter-10-extras/ex10-hai-kirei, ex10-iie-samukunai, ex10-iie-suzushikunai, ex10-hai-shizuka).
- よ is used before the deck teaches it, but its note says what it does and that it comes back in Lesson 12 (chapter-10/nikko-kirei-tokoro, chapter-10-extras/ex10-omoshiroi-tokoro).
- The こちら price card has a hint that rules out これ, which keeps it apart from the four "This one is N yen" cards I already know (chapter-10-extras/ex10-kochira-6500-en).

## Weak spots

**wrong-content**

- chapter-10/machimasu (blocker): the note calls まちます "the plain/dictionary form" of まってください. The chapter-0 note taught me the base verb is まつ, and every verb I know ends in ます.
- chapter-10/matte-kudasai (should-fix): sends me to that card "for the plain dictionary form", which repeats the error.

**ambiguous**

- chapter-10-extras/pretty (should-fix): "Pretty / also 'clean, tidy'" wants きれいな, while the base card chapter-10/kirei, "Pretty, beautiful, clean", wants きれい. I cannot tell from the front which one is being asked for.
- chapter-10-extras/ex10-hai-donata (should-fix): "Yes. Who is it?" Chapter-2-extras taught だれですか as "Who is it?", so I answer with だれ, not どなた.
- chapter-10/tokoro (polish): "Place" on the Production front fits ばしょ ("Location") as well. Only the note on the back tells them apart.

**leaks-answer**

- chapter-10-extras/f10-wain-yasukunai (polish): the hint names やすい and says "in the negative", which gives away what the front is asking for.

**missing-info**

- chapter-10/mt-fuji (polish): ふじさん looks like "Mr. Fuji" to someone who learned さん only as the honorific. A one-line note would fix it.

**note-unverifiable**

- chapter-10-extras/f10-apuri-yokunai (polish): calls いい "the one irregular い-adjective", while the same unit teaches かっこいい and says nothing about its negative.

**other**

- chapter-10/totemo (polish): the note cites a card "What a very old clock!" that does not exist (the real one is "It's a very old clock, isn't it?") and says ほんとうに is "below".
- chapter-10/hontouni (polish): the note cites "Yeah, it's really cold."; the real card reads "Yeah, it really is cold, isn't it?".
- chapter-10-extras/ex10-hontou-ni-ii-tenki-ne (polish): romaji "ee, hontō ni" here, but "ē, hontōni" on the base cards for the same words.
