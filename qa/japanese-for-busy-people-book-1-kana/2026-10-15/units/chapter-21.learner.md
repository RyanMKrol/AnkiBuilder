# Lesson 21: Asking Permission: May I Have It? (Learner)

This chapter was easy to study. The new grammar is one frame, a て-form plus もいいですか, and I already knew how to make て-forms from Lessons 18 and 19, so every permission sentence was something I could build. The base unit gives each new verb three cards (ます form, て-form, dictionary form). The body-part words come with a run of "X が いたいです" sentences that all use the same pattern. The extras reuse nouns I knew well (かいぎしつ, ちゅうしゃじょう, おてあらい, パソコン, エアコン). The ~まえに cards also carry Lesson 17's "before" pattern forward with the new dictionary forms. That is a lot of もいいですか in a row, but it is the point of the lesson. The few times I got stuck, the English named a word I had learned differently ("documents", "bag", "phone", "pay"), or two fronts read the same to me. Most of the other notes are small: a handful of hints that give away a word's meaning, and some notes that talk about the book rather than the word.

## Strong spots

- `ha-tooth` handles the clash with the topic particle は well. Its Recognition hint "a part of the body" picks the noun without saying "tooth", and the note explains the wa/ha reading.
- `kibun-ga-warui` and `lesson21-feel-sick-reply` ask for the same idea, but the scene on the second ("Replying to a doctor who has just asked what's wrong") and the です keep them apart.
- `lesson21-use-until-four-permission` and `lesson21-give-address-name-here` have scenes that make short, reply-shaped sentences answerable.
- The dictionary-form notes (`suwaru-dict-form`, `hairu-dict-form`, `oku-dict-form`, `kesu-dict-form`) state the Regular 1 change in the same words Lesson 15 used. `hairu-dict-form`'s warning that it looks like Regular 2 matches the one I already had on かえる.
- `sumimasenga-chotto` ties back to Lesson 16's trailing ちょっと, and its note says when to use it.
- The `-mae-ni-` extras (e.g. `mado-wo-akeru-mae-ni-eakon`, `keeki-wo-oku-mae-ni-shashin`) each point back to ねるまえに from Lesson 17, so the dictionary form before まえに never felt new.

## Weak spots

**Ambiguous / unanswerable Production fronts (should-fix)**

- `suwattemo-iidesu-ka` ("May I sit [here]?", すわってもいいですか) vs `ks-sit-permission` ("May I sit here?", ここにすわってもいいですか): when they come up shuffled, I can't tell which one wants ここに.
- `toshokan-de-shiryou-copy`: "documents" makes me say しょるい, but the answer is しりょう.
- `suwaru-mae-ni-nimotsu-oite`: "my bag" makes me say かばん, but the answer is にもつ.
- `charge-my-phone`, `sumaho-wo-juuden-suru-mae-ni-denwa` (polish): "my phone" makes me say けいたい, because the Lesson 2 note said スマホ means a smartphone specifically.

**Missing info (should-fix)**

- `travel-pay-by-card`: "May I pay by card?" points me to カードで, not to カードをつかって.

**Hints that give the answer away (polish)**

- `ni-particle-put-place`, `tomemasu-park`, `yasumimasu-day-off`, `hai-douzo-permission`: each Recognition hint defines the meaning the card asks for. Some cue is needed in each case, to keep the card apart from a sense I already knew, but these state the meaning outright.

**Notes about the book, not the word (polish)**

- `okimasu` ("named in GRAMMAR 2's prose") and `dou-shimashitaka` ("see Notes 2, p. 208") point to a book I don't have.
- `suwatte-sit-down-te-form` and the other seven て-form cards: "drilled in the chapter's conjugation table".
- `akemasu` (and `shimemasu`, `tsukemasu`): "Marked (R2) in the book: a ru-verb (Regular II)" uses labels the deck never taught me.
- `keshimasu`: "unlike あけます/しめます/つけます above" assumes cards come in file order.
