# Casual Style 2: Learner report

This chapter was easy to study. Nearly everything builds on something I already had: the て-form
from Lesson 18, the dictionary forms from Lesson 15, the ない-form from Lesson 22, てもいいですか from
Lesson 21, and the casual style from Casual Style 1. The base unit turns those into a clear set of
た-forms and なかった-forms, and every note told me the rule (and the exception, いく → いった)
instead of repeating the answer. The extras put each form into a short sentence with words I knew
(こうえん, てがみ, あさごはん, カメラ). The problems were all on Production fronts where a casual
English gloss lands on something an earlier card already owns.

## Strong spots

- The た-form set teaches one rule per card and states it plainly: ta-katta, ta-kaita, ta-oyoida,
  ta-hanashita, read-it-yonda, ta-tabeta. ta-itta names the one Regular 1 exception, and ta-kita and
  ta-shita link the irregular past back to きて and して.
- The なかった cards (ikanakatta-casual-past-neg, nakatta-tabenakatta, nakatta-konakatta) give one
  change that works for every verb, and nakatta-konakatta heads off the こ/き slip.
- casual-invitation-konai explains both the ない-form invitation and the dropped particle, which made
  the extras invitations (casual-invitation-koen, casual-invitation-eiga, kondo-no-doyou-uchi-konai)
  easy to read.
- The refusal cards have scenes that tell them apart: no-good-dame, dont-want-to-iyada,
  gentle-refusal-chotto, casual-sure-iiyo.
- casual-request-itte warns that いって from いう looks the same as いって from いく. That note
  answered a question before I had it.

## Weak spots

**ambiguous**

- arigatou-thanks (Production): "Thanks. (casual)" is exactly what どうも (chapter-5, doumo) was
  taught as.
- gentle-refusal-chotto (Production): "Well, that's a bit..." is nearly the gloss of bare ちょっと
  (chapter-16, chotto-decline), so nothing asks for the えー.

**unanswerable**

- ore-wa-mada (Production): "(male speaker)" fits ぼく as well as おれ.
- ta-itta (Recognition, polish): bare いった could be "said" from いう. Its siblings ta-kita and
  ta-shita have scenes and it does not.

**missing-info**

- atashi-wa-ikanai (Production, polish): it keeps は where the Casual Style 1 twin おれ、いかない drops
  it, and no note says why.

**note-unverifiable**

- dont-want-to-iyada (polish): "Stronger than だめ" is a ranking I can't check, and the だめ card
  calls だめ a firm, flat refusal.
