# Chapter 07: Lesson 5: A Trip to Okinawa (chapter-6), learner pass

This chapter was easy to study, in a good way. It has 32 single kanji and 34 words, and every word
breaks down into kanji I either met in an earlier unit or meet as a kanji card here. I checked each
one against the ledger and this unit's kanji cards: 宿題 is 宿 + 題, 面白い is 面 + 白, 一緒に is
一 (ch5, "it-") + 緒, 大丈夫 is 大 + 丈 + 夫, お土産 is 土 (ch4) + 産, 天気 is 天 (ch5) + 気, 部屋 is
部 + 屋 (ch5). The kun stems on the kanji cards line up with the words: atara for 新しい, isoga for
忙しい, muzuka for 難しい, kowa for 怖い, oyo for 泳ぐ, no for 乗る, de for 出かける, kira for 嫌い.
Where a word needs a reading no kanji card gave me (kuda in 果物, he in 部屋, miyage in お土産, bu in
大丈夫), the back's romaji teaches it, which is the deck's
pattern and works. No front repeats one I already know, so nothing collides. I found nothing worth a
line.

## Strong spots

- The two "atsui" words are told apart on the back, and so are their kanji: 暑い "Hot (weather)"
  against 熱い "Hot (thing)" (r-2f7806f919a5, r-51bcc9d34833), with 暑 "Hot (weather)" and 熱 "Heat;
  fever" (r-f5a408ddfe5b, r-dffabaa810c5). 寒い adds "(weather, not used for things)"
  (r-bc0d3ddc48ec) and 古い "(thing, not used for people)" (r-ae8f14fe0b81), so the limits are on
  the card where I need them.
- The like/dislike set reads as one family: 好 / 好き / 大好き and 嫌 / 嫌い / 大嫌い
  (r-27e4fe4c3fe2, r-991f96a8ce57, r-21bb71d48c26, r-7b59b16db1db, r-adb7c9ef10fd, r-ed9d672c6023).
- Kana words I already had now get their kanji with the same meaning, which is a nice confirmation:
  暇 "Not busy; free (time)" matches ひまな (r-ec7f1ae42b7a), 静か matches しずかな (r-c9bef118f612),
  元気 matches げんきな (r-ba1a3a1305a2).
- 食べ物 and 飲み物 (r-caf1f189d706, r-4630a7dd90bb) build on 食べる and 飲む from ch4, and 誕生日
  (r-b981696f046b) I could read straight off 天皇誕生日 from ch5.
