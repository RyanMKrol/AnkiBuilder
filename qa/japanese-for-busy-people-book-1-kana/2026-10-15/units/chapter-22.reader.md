## Findings

**book-gap**

- `chapter-22/toranaide-kudasai` (should-fix): the book's register note for ないでください (a fairly
  strong expression, softened by giving a reason) is on no card, and the base note calls the request
  simply "polite".

**note-unverifiable**

- `chapter-22/osake` (polish): the note says さけ without お "often means Japanese rice wine in
  particular". The book says only "(お)さけ alcoholic drink, sake", and the distinction is not
  reliable: both forms are used for alcohol in general and for sake.
