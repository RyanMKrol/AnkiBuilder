# Chapter 06: Lesson 4: The First Date (chapter-5), Learner

This chapter is a big jump in volume: 90 single kanji and 71 words, most of them calendar words
(months, weekdays, relative time words) and the full set of Japanese public holidays. It studies
well. The kanji cards come before or alongside the words built from them, and nearly every compound
can be read from the kanji cards the learner has just met: 月曜日 from 月 and the chapter-4 曜 and
日, 郵便局 from 郵, 便 and 局, 図書館 from 図, 書 and 館. The kana words from the kana chapter
(ぎんこう, としょかん, ゆうびんきょく, だいがく, こうこう, せんせい) make the kanji forms easy to
confirm. The few problems are small: one romaji with a stray tilde, one holiday English that says
more than the front, one kanji gloss that points the wrong way in time, and two whole-word readings
the back does not mark as special.

## Strong spots

- The month series (r-313fd5bb330e 一月 through r-37b5ae5f0edd 十二月) gets the irregular readings
  right and the number kanji cards list them: 四月 shi-gatsu, 七月 shichi-gatsu, 九月 ku-gatsu.
- The relative time grid is complete and consistent: 先週/今週/来週/再来週, 先月/今月/来月/再来月,
  去年/今年/来年/再来年. The 再 card (r-95fd8be0d991, "Again; twice") makes the さらい words make
  sense.
- Holidays are fully decomposable from the chapter's own kanji cards: 勤労感謝の日 (r-90236f3e4722)
  from 勤, 労, 感, 謝; 憲法記念日 (r-a7fc025fa5fd) from 憲, 法, 記, 念; 天皇誕生日
  (r-234974a6f082) from 天, 皇, 誕, 生.
- Word/kanji pairs read cleanly as separate cards: 買 and 買う, 書 and 書く, 会 and 会う, 待 and
  待つ, 撮 and 撮る, 近 and 近く.
- こどもの日 (r-d4309788b65d) is written in kana while 子供 (r-11fb5d289abc) has its own card, so
  the learner sees both spellings without confusion.

## Weak spots

**wrong-content (polish)**

- r-09558b2cc685 時間: back says "Says: ~jikan" and "Time; ... hours (duration)". The tilde is not
  part of the word and the counter slot is not on the front; 一時間 (r-0ce8b690e987) says
  "ichi-jikan" cleanly.
- r-40ebd4ed4e1a スポーツの日: "Health and Sports Day". The front reads "Sports Day"; nothing on it
  says "Health".

**missing-info (polish)**

- r-82d45ef2106f 先: glossed only "Ahead", but 先月 and 先週 in this chapter mean "last". A learner
  reading those cold guesses "next".
- r-fe9ea3f07077 今年 (kotoshi) and r-b43054fed828 昨日 (kinō): neither reading can be built from
  the kanji cards (今 kon/ima, 年 nen/toshi, 昨 saku), and the back does not say these are
  whole-word readings. Same pattern as 今日 and 明日 in chapter-4.
