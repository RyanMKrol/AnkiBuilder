# Lesson 14, as the learner (chapter-14 + chapter-14-extras)

This chapter is good to study. It has one clear job, getting the direction of a gift or loan right
(あげます, もらいます, くれます, かします, かります), and it does that job with paired cards that tell the
same event from both sides. The notes keep pointing at the one thing that trips you up, that に marks
the receiver with あげます and the giver with もらいます, and by the end of the extras I could answer the
sentence cards from the verb alone. The new "have" sense of あります is introduced carefully on the base
card and practised well in the extras. The problems are mostly small. Two words compete for "scarf",
"bag" and "evening" each point at a word other than the one the card wants, one note teaches a false
rule about colour adjectives, and a few notes point at things a shuffled learner cannot see.

## Strong spots

- The give/receive mirror pairs: ks1-ageru-hana / ks2-morau-hana, kasu-kasa-a / kariru-kasa-b,
  x14b-yubiwa-agemashita-ni / x14b-yubiwa-moraimashita-ni, x14b-kuruma-kashimasu-ashita /
  x14b-kuruma-karimasu-ashita. Each one names its twin in full, so the comparison still works in a
  shuffle.
- くれます is taught by contrast, and taught well: x14b-nakamura-hana-kuremashita names the classic
  mistake outright, x14b-emma-chiketto-kuremashita gives a one-line test, and
  x14b-tsuma-iyaringu-kuremashita extends the rule to the speaker's family.
- Particle notes that resolve a real doubt: x14b-risa-chokoreeto-kuremashita (two に with two jobs),
  x14b-okane-ga-arimasen (が, never を), x14b-kinou-yotei-arimasendeshita (ありませんでした vs
  じゃありませんでした).
- Hints that separate a new sense from an old one: jikan ("on its own, as a noun" against じかん
  "hours"), arimasu-have ("about owning something"), baggu ("loanword" against かばん and ふくろ),
  ni-from-particle (against から "From").
- Loanword false friends get notes worth having: sweatshirt (トレーナー) and hoodie (パーカー).
- Answer cards carry a scene naming their question (concert-ticket-a, wine-received-a,
  christmas-when-received-a, fib-nakamura-san-ni-karimasu), so none of them is a guess.

## Weak spots

**wrong-content**

- eg-kuroi-beruto (blocker): the note says くろい, あかい, あおい and しろい are the only い-adjective
  colours, "those four and no others". The learner already has ちゃいろい and きいろい on earlier cards.

**ambiguous / unanswerable**

- scarf (should-fix): "Scarf" fits both スカーフ and マフラー, since マフラー is glossed "Muffler, scarf"
  and its note says the two mean the same thing. The same problem shows up on kuremasu-example and
  x14-suteki-na-sukaafu-desune, both polish. Narrowing the マフラー gloss fixes all three.
- x14b-suteki-na-baggu-doko-de (should-fix): "lovely bag" pulls toward かばん; the card wants バッグ.
- x14-ashita-no-yoru-jikan-arimasuka (should-fix): "tomorrow evening" pulls toward ばん ("Evening");
  the card wants よる ("Night").

**missing-info**

- x14-sukaafu-ga-arimasu-yo (should-fix): no owner and no scene, so it reads "there's a scarf". The
  note also calls it "the chapter's first use", which means nothing in a shuffle.
- arimasen, arimashita, arimasendeshita (polish): the bare conjugated forms drop the "about owning
  something" hint, so "there isn't / there was" are equally good answers.

**note-unverifiable**

- x14-dare-ni-moraimashita-q (should-fix): "だれに, not だれから" rules out から, and nothing the learner
  has been taught says it is wrong.

**note-restates-card**

- iindesuka (repeats its own hint), kyouto-omiyage (glosses two words already known). Both polish.

**other**

- ni-from-particle: points to "GRAMMAR 1–2", which is in the book, not the deck.
- ks2-morau-hana: "Compare with the previous card".
- yoku: hint "about how skillfully" does not fit "suits you well".
- fib-emma-douryou-ni-agemasu: どうりょう takes あげます right after a note says a gift to "my colleague"
  takes くれます.
- fib-chichi-itsu-okane-kuremasu-q: asks about the asker's own father, while its answer card is about
  Smith-san's father.
- fib-suteki-mafuraa: romaji "mafuraa" against the vocabulary card's "mafurā".
- x14b-okane-kashimasendeshita: "-masen deshita" is split in the extras but written as one word in the
  base unit.
