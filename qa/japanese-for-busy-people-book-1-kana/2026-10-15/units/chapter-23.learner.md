# Lesson 23 (Explaining Actions: What Are You Doing Now?): the Learner

This chapter felt easy to study, and that is a good thing. The ています form arrives on things I already know: the て-forms from Lesson 18 and verbs like のみます, はなします and たべます. So のんでいます and はなしていません make sense the moment I turn them over. The もう / まだです pair and the new words (じゅんび, しりょうができました, おわります) come back again and again in short, believable office sentences, and the scene lines on answer cards (asked whether the report is ready, asked where Emma-san is) do their job without giving the answer away. Most of the trouble comes from one place. The base note teaches ています as "an action happening right now", but the extras also use it for habits ("takes guitar lessons every week", "sometimes goes jogging"). In English those sentences look exactly like the Lesson 17 cards whose answer was ならいます, so on Production I keep giving the ます form. A smaller group of cards asks for the "X の チェック / せつめい を します" construction when I was taught a different and equally good way to say the same thing.

## Strong spots

- The ています / ていません pairs (tsukutte-imasu-be-making, tsukutte-imasen-2, nonde-imasu-2, nonde-imasen-2, hanashite-imasu-2, hanashite-imasen-2, setsumei-shite-imasu-2, setsumei-shite-imasen-2) build directly on Lesson 18's て-forms and need nothing new. tsukutte-imasen-2's note ("ています → ていません, the same way います negates") is short and enough.
- The もう family is handled carefully. mousugu-soon and mou-sukoshi each warn that they are fixed phrases and not "already + X", and the extras give each one a real use (mousugu-soon-1, presenting-prep-wait).
- Noun / verb pairs are kept apart by a hint: sunoboodo-snowboard / snowboard-o-shimasu, benkyou-study ("the noun"), download-loanword ("the noun"), nanika-before-noun ("placed before a noun", against Lesson 16's なにか "something"), yuugata-evening ("early evening, around dusk", against ばん).
- Answer cards carry the question as a scene: writing-report-wait, meeting-materials-ready-a, on-the-phone-now, explaining-project-not-materials, cleaning-finished-a, already-eaten-lunch-a, emma-in-meeting-room, dialogue-dry-cleaning-ready-wednesday-evening. Each can be answered alone, and none gives the answer away.
- contrast-ni-wa-tanaka-yamada and contrast-snowboard-not-ski have notes that actually teach: when は replaces を, and when it is added after に.

## Weak spots

**ambiguous**

- kato-guitar-weekly, shodou-calligraphy-1, nakamura-flower-arrangement-weekly, suzuki-french-weekly (should-fix): "X-san takes Y lessons every week." is the same English as chapter-17-extras/naraimasu-1, whose answer was ならいます. Here the answer is ならっています, and the front gives no sign of it.
- check-noun (should-fix): Production "Check / looking something over, not a bill" reads like the Lesson 17 verb チェックします ("To check"). It has no "the noun" hint, although download-loanword and benkyou-study do.
- ima-ii-desu-ka (should-fix): nearly the same English as chapter-18-extras/yoroshii-ima いまよろしいですか, with no cue for いい over よろしい.
- report-check-shimasu (should-fix): "I will check the report." I was taught ニュースをチェックします, so I answer レポートをチェックします. The card wants レポートのチェックをします.
- souji-shimasu-clean (polish): "Clean" also fits きれい ("Pretty, beautiful, clean").
- moshi-moshi-1 (polish): "Hello, this is Tanaka." matches chapter-6-extras/td-hai-chan-desu, whose answer was はい + name.

**unanswerable**

- jogging-o-shimasu-1 (should-fix): "Smith-san sometimes goes jogging." is a habit. Every habit I know takes ます, including undou-o-shimasu-1 in this same unit ("exercises every morning" → うんどうをします). The card wants しています.
- check-noun-1 (polish): "Please check over the materials." fits しりょうをチェックしてください as well as the おねがいします answer.

**wrong-content**

- kai-floor-counter (should-fix): the note says "Only 3 voices it to がい", but I already learned なんがい ("which floor?") in Lesson 5.

**missing-info**

- tsukutte-imasu-be-making (polish): the only note on ています limits it to "right now". It does not cover the ongoing or habitual uses the extras rely on.
- smith-explains-project (polish): no card explains "X の [noun] を します" (プロジェクトのせつめい, パーティーのじゅんび), though about ten cards use it.

**untaught-prerequisite**

- yoyaku-suru-dict-form-1 (polish): ends in でんわします. Every earlier card has でんわをします.

**other**

- dekimasu-be-done-2 (polish): the note "(R2) marks its conjugation class in the book" talks about a label that is not on the card.
- shodou-calligraphy-1 (polish): the note "Follows the chapter's EXERCISES III drill pattern directly" points to a book exercise I don't have.
- Unit (polish): notes on mou-already, madadesu-not-yet, mousugu-soon, mou-sukoshi and the four dictionary-form cards say "above" / "below", which means nothing in a shuffled deck.

Preflight's items (the four long Production faces, the note-claim and reviewNote notices, the corpus drift) are not repeated here.
