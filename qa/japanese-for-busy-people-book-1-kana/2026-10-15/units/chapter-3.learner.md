# Lesson 3: Asking the Time: What Time Is It? (learner pass)

Units: `chapter-3` (104 cards), then `chapter-3-extras` (75 cards).

This chapter felt like a drill book, in a good way. The base unit hands me the numbers 10 to 90,
every hour from 1:00 to 12:00, a full run of 3:05 to 3:55, and then から and まで, and the extras
turn all of it into short questions and answers about when shops, meetings and parties open and
close. The irregular hours (くじ, よじ, しちじ) each have a note saying which number reading they
replace, which is exactly what I needed, and nearly every short answer has a scene naming the
question it answers. The problems are small and cluster in a few places: two answer cards drop
"from" from the English while the Japanese keeps から, three sentences suddenly write the hour as a
digit, フロント is glossed with the same English as うけつけ from Lesson 1, and a couple of particle
hints on the Recognition side give the meaning away.

## Strong spots

- **Irregular hour notes** (`ku-ji`, `yo-ji`, `shichi-ji`): each names the Lesson 2 number it does
  not use (きゅう, よん, なな), so the trap is pointed out on the card that sets it.
- **Irregular minute notes in the extras** (`one-minute`, `six-minutes`, `eight-minutes`,
  `ten-minutes`): each says what the number turns into before ぷん.
- **から / まで minimal pairs** (`resutoran-nanji-kara-q` with `resutoran-nanji-made-q`,
  `jimu-gozen-kuji-kara`, `shigoto-goji-made`, `rasuto-oodaa-niji-desu`): the notes contrast the
  two particles and plain です, which is the hardest idea in the chapter, and they do it well.
- **Short answers with scenes** (`fib-c3-meeting-end-a`, `td-last-order-a`, `mise-kuji-kara-a`,
  `ima-nanji-a` and most others): I always knew which question I was answering.
- **いつ vs なんじ** (`nanji`, `itsu`, `yasumi-ashita-a`, `kaigi-itsu-q`): the notes mirror each
  other and explain why a day alone can answer いつですか.
- **The compound notes** on `asa`, `gohan` and `ban` mention あさごはん and ばんごはん, so the extras
  cards that use them (`hotel-asagohan-q`, `bangohan-rokuji-kara`) did not come out of nowhere.

## Weak spots

**wrong-content**

- `fib-c3-presentation-a` and `fib-c3-party-a` (should-fix): the English reads "It's at 3:00 today."
  and "It's at 5:00 tomorrow." but the answers end からです. I said きょうのさんじです, which the
  extras' own note on `rasuto-oodaa-niji-desu` says means something different. The extras sibling
  `sumisu-purezen-a` gets it right with "It's from 3:00 P.M. today."
- `sou-desu-ka` (polish): the note says そうです confirms "your own statement", but Lesson 1 taught
  it as confirming what the other person asked.

**ambiguous**

- `furonto` (should-fix) and `furonto-q` (polish): "Reception desk" is the gloss of うけつけ from
  Lesson 1, and only the back's note says フロント is the hotel desk.
- `resutoran` (polish): "Restaurant" also fits みせ ("Restaurant, shop").
- `ranchitaimu` (polish): "Lunchtime" against ひるやすみ's "Lunch break".

**leaks-answer** (Recognition side)

- `made-particle` (should-fix): the hint contains the word "until".
- `kara-particle`, `yasumi` (polish): the hints "a starting time" and "a pause during the day"
  paraphrase the answer.

**missing-info**

- `san-ji-sanjuppun-han` (polish): bare "3:30" in a run of spelled-out minutes invites
  さんじさんじゅっぷん; the other はん cards say "half past" in the English.
- `fun-pun-suffix` (polish): says ふん and ぷん alternate "depending on the preceding sound" but
  not which numbers take ぷん.
- `three-minutes`, `four-minutes` (polish): no note on why ぷん, unlike the other irregular minutes.

**other**

- `fib-c3-gym-open-7` (should-fix), `fib-c3-restaurant-open-8`, `fib-c3-supermarket-10-6` (polish):
  the hours are written as digits (７じ, ８じ, １０じ, ６じ) where every other card uses kana. On the
  first one the digit hides the しち reading; I read it as なな.
- `ima-kuji-desu` (polish): the note is about being "Member 1 of a set", which means nothing in a
  shuffle (its siblings carry "Member 2" and "Member 3" too, alongside real content).
- `kaigi-ashita-kuji-a`, `ima-nanji-q` (polish): notes cite "GRAMMAR 3" and "GRAMMAR 1", which I
  do not have.
- `yasumi-itsu-q` (polish): uses やすみ for a day off, while the word card's hint says "a pause
  during the day".

**note-unverifiable**

- `shichi-ji` (polish): "7:00 keeps an older reading of 7".
- `toukyou-ima-gogo-yoji-han` (polish): 4:30 P.M. Tokyo is "the same moment" as 8:30 A.M. London,
  true only in British summer time.
