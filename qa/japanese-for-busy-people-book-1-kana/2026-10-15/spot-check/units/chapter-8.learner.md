# Lesson 8 (chapter-8 + chapter-8-extras): Learner spot-check

This chapter studied well. It adds a lot at once: を, the place sense of で, four forms each of
eight verbs, the people counter, and a restaurant phone call. But the cards keep each new piece
on its own before putting it into a sentence, and the cues do their job. The two senses of で are
pulled apart on the particle card and again on `p-kaado-de-kasa-kaimashita`. The counter cards
(`sannin`, `hitori`, `futari` against `fib-sannin-desu`, `v-hitori-desu`, `ex-futari-desu`) use
scenes to say whether a bare count or a reply is wanted. The three "how many people" questions
(`nannin`, `v-nannin-desu-ka`, `nanmeisama-desu-ka`) each have a cue that picks one. I found
nothing that teaches something false. Most of the short replies (`v-toshokan-de-shimasu`,
`v-hakone-de-shimashita`, `ex-resutoran-de-tabemasu`) come with a scene naming the question they
answer, so they can be studied on their own.

## Strong spots

- Every particle contrast comes with a note that compares it with a sentence the learner already
  knows: `p-resutoran-de-tabemasu` vs `p-resutoran-ni-ikimasu`, `p-raamen-mo-tabemashita`,
  `p-suzuki-mo-kabuki-mimasu`.
- The hints on the verb-form cards (`shimasu`, `shimasen`, `shimashita`,
  `shimasendeshita`, `shigoto-wo-shimasu`, `kaimono-wo-shimasu`) tell the noun "work"/"shop"
  apart from the verb, and they only show on the Production front.
- `fib-shuumatsu-nani-shimashitaka` and `p102-senshuumatsu-nani-o-shimashita-ka` use paired hints
  to separate しゅうまつ from せんしゅうまつ.
- New words met only inside a sentence (`p102-eigakan-ni-ikimashita`,
  `p102-nichiyoubi-kaji-o-shimasu`) are explained in the note on that card.

## Weak spots

**unanswerable**

- `chapter-8-extras/sp-onamae-to-odenwabangou` (should-fix): "Please give me your name and your
  telephone number." is the Lesson 4 gloss of ください word for word, so the learner answers with
  ください and not the target's おねがいします.

**leaks-answer**

- `chapter-8/kikimasu` (polish): the Recognition scene "music, the radio, the news" all but says
  "listen to".
- `chapter-8/particle-wo` (polish): the Recognition scene "marking the thing the verb acts on" is
  the answer.

**ambiguous**

- `chapter-8/mimasu` (polish): the bare "See" also fits わかります ("Understand, see, get it"),
  and both cards are in the same category.

**other**

- `chapter-8-extras/td-doyoubi-tenpura-tabemasu` (polish): the note's word-order template
  ("person は — time に — place で — object を") does not match the card, which has no は and
  has a と phrase.

Preflight's own items (the ii-desu-ne answerability, romaji hyphenation, the long Production face
on td-doyoubi-tenpura-tabemasu, the hamburger loanword, the たべもの and ます notes) were not
reported again.
