**Grammar (8)**

1. G1 て-form formation: Regular 1 by the sound before ます, Regular 2 and Irregular change ます to て.
2. G2 person は V1-て、V2: consecutive actions, same subject.
3. G2 two or three clauses can be chained; the first and second verbs are in て-form.
4. G2 clauses whose mood or tense differ cannot be linked with て (statement + suggestion example).
5. G2 after a verb of movement (いきます, きます, かえります) the second action is assumed to happen at that place (きのう ぎんざに いって、ひるごはんを たべました).
6. G3 noun の あと、verb.
7. G3 あとで: で is added to stress "after, not before".
8. SPEAKING PRACTICE NOTES: a noun made the topic with は; が and を change to は, other particles take は on top; the topic moves to the head of the sentence (ステーキは すきですか, ばんごはんは かいぎの まえに たべますか).

**Key sentences (2)**: エマさんは あした おおさかししゃに いって、チャンさんに あいます / スミスさんは かいぎの あと、レポートを かきました.
