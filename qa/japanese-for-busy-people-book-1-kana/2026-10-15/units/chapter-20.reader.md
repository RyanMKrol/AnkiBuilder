## Findings

**book-gap**

- `chapter-20/kakarimasu-2` (should-fix): G3 says かかります is for a time the speaker feels is long,
  and "period です" is used when it is not. The card gives the first half only, and no card gives
  the second.
- `chapter-20` unit (polish): G3 and G4 both say the period goes straight before かかります / います
  with no particle after it. Sentences show it; no back says it.

**untaught-prerequisite**

- `chapter-20-extras/yasumi-ni-nebou-suru-no-ga-suki` (should-fix): やすみのひ needs ひ "day", which
  no earlier card teaches. The only ひ in the ledger is "Fire".

Not re-reported (preflight already raises them): the corpus-drift ids, the romaji notes on
`bansen-counter`, `jikan-hours`, `ikitai-n-desu-ga` and the `yasumi-ni-nebou` note, the production
length infos, and the composition claim in the `yasumi-ni-nebou` note.
