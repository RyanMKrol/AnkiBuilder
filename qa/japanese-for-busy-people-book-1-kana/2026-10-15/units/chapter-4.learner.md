# Lesson 4 (chapter-4 + chapter-4-extras): learner pass

A clean, easy chapter to study. It adds それ and あれ to the これ I already had, then いくら, the big numbers (ひゃく, せん, まん, おく, ちょう), えん, も, と, で and the two ways of ordering (をください and をおねがいします). Almost every sentence in the extras is built from words I knew or met a few cards earlier: tablets, televisions, fridges, coffee and sandwiches, priced and pointed at. The English keeps the three distances apart ("this one", "that one", "that one over there") on every card, so I never had to guess which pronoun was wanted. Short replies get a scene ("answering how much this one costs", "answering whether that's a mouse"), and the number cards are plain and unambiguous. What tripped me up was ordering: the deck teaches ください and おねがいします as two ways to ask for a thing, but only one pair of cards tells me which one a front wants, and one card breaks the pattern the others follow. Beyond that, a run of extras notes mentions "sets", "Member 3" and "GRAMMAR 2", none of which a learner can see.

## Strong spots

- **Distance kept clear in every English.** ks1-sore-smartphone, ks1-are-tablet, sore-wa-ikura-desu-ka, are-wa-ikura-desu-ka, sore-o-misete-kudasai, are-o-misete-kudasai, kore-wa-sumaho-desu. The notes on sore-wa-ikura-desu-ka ("これ for what is in your own hand, それ for what the salesperson is holding, あれ for what is across the shop") and are-o-misete-kudasai say it in shop terms.
- **Number sound changes explained once and consistently.** num-300, num-10000, num-100, sore-wa-sen-en-desu (no いち before せん or ひゃく, but いちまん), kore-wa-hassen-en-desu, denshi-renji-wa-nimankyusen-happyaku-en-desu (read in blocks, largest first).
- **も placement.** sore-mo-sen-en-desu (も takes は's place, それはも is impossible), kore-mo-kudasai and juice-mo-onegaishimasu (も takes を's place), sore-mo-hassen-en-ja-arimasen (same particle in a negative reads "either"), iie-pasokon-wa-goman-en-ja-arimasen (question with も, correction with は).
- **と as a block.** to-toaster-microwave-kudasai and to-sandwich-coffee-ikura-q: one を or one は after the joined pair.
- **The one ordering pair that does it right.** kohi-o-kudasai and kohi-o-onegaishimasu carry hints ("the blunter of the two requests" / "the wider-use polite request") that make each front answerable.
- **Scenes on short replies.** gohyaku-en-desu, hyakunanajuu-en-desu, iie-taburetto-desu, fib-iie-rimokon-desu, iie-are-wa-gosen-en-ja-arimasen, hai-douzo (where はい is "here", not "yes").

## Weak spots

**ambiguous**

- chapter-4/ks3-kore-kudasai (should-fix): "I would like this one, please." wants これをください, but "I would like ..., please" is おねがいします on ks4-curry-salad and kohi-o-onegaishimasu. Every other ください card says "I'll take ..." or "X, please."
- chapter-4-extras/tokei-o-misete-kudasai, chapter-4-extras/kore-mo-sore-mo-tokei (polish): "watch" is the whole gloss of うでどけい (chapter-2-extras), so とけい is not the only answer.

**unanswerable**

- chapter-4/ks4-curry-salad and, as one unit finding, chapter-4-extras orange-juice-o-kudasai, sumimasen-sandoitchi-to-kohi, to-koucha-choko-keki, sarada-mo-onegaishimasu, juice-mo-onegaishimasu (polish): each English fits both をください and をおねがいします and carries no hint. A learner who works out the unstated rule ("I'll take" / "X, please" is ください, "I would like" is おねがいします) can answer them, but nothing states the rule, and ks3-kore-kudasai breaks it.

**leaks-answer**

- chapter-4/to-particle, chapter-4/de-particle (polish): the Recognition front shows the hint ("joining two nouns into a list", "paying, or doing something by a method"), which is the meaning the card asks for. Each particle has only one use so far, so the hint has nothing to separate.

**wrong-content**

- chapter-4-extras/kohi-o-kudasai (polish): the note says ください "cannot ask for a service", but みせてください, おしえてください and ちょっとまってください do exactly that. True only of noun + をください.
- chapter-4-extras/fib-iie-rimokon-desu (polish): romaji "iie" where every other card writes "īe".

**untaught-prerequisite**

- chapter-4/mo-particle (polish): the note says も replaces は "or が (ga)". No card has taught が.

**other**

- chapter-4/ja (should-fix): the hint "the casual contraction" contradicts the note, which says this じゃ is _not_ the じゃ of じゃありません, the one it calls the contraction. I came away unsure what this じゃ is.
- Notes that point at things a learner cannot see (polish): chapter-4-extras/are-wa-sumaho-ja-arimasen ("the スマホ distance set"), kore-wa-hassen-en-desu-ka ("Member 3 of the 8,000えん set"), kore-wa-hassen-en-ja-arimasen ("Member 2"), kore-wa-anata-no-kasa-desu-ka ("Completes the かさ set"), sore-mo-watashi-no-desu, kore-wa-watashi-no-kasa-desu ("GRAMMAR 2"), ja-kore-to-sore-o-kudasai ("GRAMMAR 4"), and chapter-4/en-suffix ("in the source").

I have not repeated anything preflight already reports: the uncarded forms named in the misete-kudasai, num-8000, num-800, num-600, num-3000, num-0.7 and num-1-2 notes; the answer-shaped English on fib-c4-tokyo-seoul-10am and fib-c4-13000-yen-price; the inline romaji on card and the two hassen-en notes; the scene claim on iie-pasokon-wa-goman-en-ja-arimasen; the "◇ one is ◇ yen" near siblings; and the terminal-punctuation romaji.
