# Chapter 18: Reading and Writing 4: Mary's Weekend (chapter-17), Learner

This was an easy, pleasant chapter to study. Only three kanji are new (粘, 半, 額), and nearly every
word is built from kanji I already read: 水 gives 水泳, 水着 and 水道, 火 and 山 give 火山, 上 and 屋
give 屋上, and 半 runs through 半分, 半額, 半年 and 三時半. The new kanji pay off inside the same
chapter (粘 in 粘土, 額 in 半額), so a card I fail on the shuffle is usually explained by another one I
have just seen. The backs are short and clear. My only real stumble was 三日, where what I had been
taught led me to the wrong sense.

## Strong spots

- The 半 family: r-2bff6583b69a 半, r-a6986d6965af 半分, r-d84accc4d4cc 半額, r-1ebc6f417104 半年 and
  r-82da0ea22676 三時半 all read cleanly from 半 plus a kanji I know, and 三時半 lines up with はん
  "Half (past the hour)" and 三時 from earlier.
- New kanji used straight away: r-300c9caa9cf8 粘 then r-f8da62321db4 粘土; r-e6f8cf1c2c7e 額 then
  半額.
- Words that a reader can work out from their parts and then confirm on the back: r-67cd316207a7
  水泳, r-62ac794272dd 水着, r-7978de3ee7d0 水道, r-c18a3754bb5f 火山, r-0b8360d04a9f 花火,
  r-a2090e26b758 屋上, r-010108fbb92b 土地, r-622f94723b62 一年中.
- r-c12140a0ffb4 日本語 and r-498a60e1125d 日本人 give the kanji spelling of words I first met in kana.

## Weak spots

**Unanswerable**

- r-482dccb7367c 三日 "Three days" (should-fix). The kana deck taught むいか as "The 6th (day of the
  month)", and the duration "two days" came to me as 二日間, with 間. So on 三日 I answer "the 3rd",
  and the back tells me I am wrong. Both senses belong on the back.

**Other**

- r-01d06413bb6c 下手な and r-b4b8a1a29dfc 上手な (polish). I already read 下手 and 上手 from
  Lesson 8. These are the same written words with な added, so they are repeat reviews rather than
  new reading.

Already known from preflight and not repeated here: 中学 and 中学校 both answer "Junior high school"
(acknowledged).
