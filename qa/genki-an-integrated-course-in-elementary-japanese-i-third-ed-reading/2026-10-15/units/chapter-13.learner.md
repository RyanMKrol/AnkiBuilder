# Chapter 14: Lesson 12: Feeling Sick (chapter-13), Learner

A medical chapter: 46 new single kanji, then 54 words built from them, most of them symptoms, illnesses
and the kinds of clinic. It studied well. Nearly every word is spelled from kanji I had just met on
their own card (皮 and 膚 for 皮膚科, 骨 and 折 for 骨折, 花 plus the new 粉 and 症 for 花粉症), so each
word card felt like a payoff rather than a new thing to memorise. The trouble spots are all the same
small kind: a handful of words read a kanji in a way its own card never taught, and the back does not
say so. Nothing in the unit teaches anything false or cannot be studied.

## Strong spots

- The new kanji are all put to work in this same unit: 計 in 体温計 (r-552df3d1c1e6), 張 in 緊張する
  (r-dcb12839608e), 形 in 整形外科 (r-d3df969e0182), 氏 in 彼氏 (r-e9e796e9cb60), 合 in 試合
  (r-3573405f6923), 興 in 興味 (r-64ee22460455). No single kanji is left hanging.
- Kanji I already had make the hard words easy: 洗 from Lesson 8 for お手洗い (r-73e6684ff80e), 術
  from Lesson 11 for 手術 (r-abd2848ba629), 質 from Lesson 11 for 抗生物質 (r-1f5337f35438), 別 from
  Lesson 7 for 別れる (r-868fe3661f86).
- Opposite pairs arrive together and are glossed as a pair: 広い "Wide; spacious" and 狭い "Narrow; not
  spacious" (r-14a64db61704, r-6c36eb6f1079).
- The near neighbours are told apart on the back: 目医者 "Eye doctor" against 眼科 "Ophthalmology (eye
  clinic)" (r-cac95c7e2427, r-c6512529b527), 歯医者 "Dentist" against 歯科 "Dentistry (dental clinic)"
  (r-0ab9b459d777, r-7878d7a46305), 皮 "Skin; peel" against 膚 "Skin (of the body)" (r-445ba2f95952,
  r-2a46684e2db2).
- 渇く spells out "(of the throat) to feel thirsty" (r-ac3f035949bf), which is the use I would need.

## Weak spots

**missing-info (6, all polish)**

- A reading the kanji card never taught, with no word on the back that it is new: 下痢 geri, where 下
  only taught shita (r-a0e2a8fa1a60); 歯科 shika, where 歯 only taught ha and 歯医者 in the same unit
  reads haisha (r-7878d7a46305); 彼女 kanojo, where 彼 in this same unit teaches only kare
  (r-4325ce7f3fa1).
- A whole-word reading the back does not flag: 二日酔い futsukayoi, which 二 (ni / futa) cannot give
  and 二日 was never carded (r-81a4cd3110dd); 風邪 kaze, where 邪 is silent (r-7e63ca48451d).
- 内科 "Physician" (r-003253e63d95) reads as a synonym of the 医者 "Doctor" I already know, and the
  -科 set switches between the person (外科 "Surgeon", 皮膚科 "Dermatologist") and the department (眼科,
  歯科), so I could not tell what 内科 is.

**wrong-content (1, polish)**

- 代 (r-b5a9258cf7d9) says "~dai" with a tilde the front does not have, and "Charge; fee" gives no slot
  marker, the same tilde pattern already reported for Lessons 4 and 10.

Not reported: the hyphenated reading fragments on 合 ("gat-, kat-"), already raised on the Lesson 3
kanji cards, and the 18 excluded cards preflight lists for the unit.
