# Lesson 6: Going Places (1), learner pass

Lesson 6 is the first lesson with verbs, and it studied well. Everything rests on two verbs, いきます and きます, in four forms each (present, negative, past, past negative), plus the particles に, へ, と and から for where, with whom and from where. The cards build that up in small steps: the bare verb, then a one-line answer, then a full sentence with a time word, a companion and a destination. The time words (せんしゅう to らいねん, おととい, あさって) are plain one-word cards with notes that show the せん／こん／らい pattern and where it breaks. The いきます／きます point of view is explained again and again from different angles, and by the end I could tell which verb a sentence needed. The problems are few and mostly small. A handful of Production fronts accept a second answer I already know (どれ for どの, the Lesson 5 order for どれが, じゃ for では, ください for おねがいします). Three へ sentences never ask me for へ. And as in earlier extras units, several notes cite book sections or number a card inside a set I never see.

## Strong spots

- The four-form drills (chapter-6-extras/form-kuukou-_, form-tokyo-shisha-_, p102-kaisha-ni-*, kimasen, kimasen-deshita) change one thing at a time, and the notes on the past negative ("present negative plus でした") are short and right.
- いきます versus きます: chapter-6/ikimasu and kimasu set the rule, and chapter-6-extras/td-ashita-sochira-ni-ikimasu, ex9-ashita-ikimasu, hitori-de-ikimasu-ka, td-hitori-de-kimasu-ka and vocab-kazoku-kotoshi-nihon-ni-kimasu each show it from a different speaker's position. This was the hardest idea in the lesson and the cards made it stick.
- に versus へ done right: chapter-6-extras/he-eki-ni-ikimasu and he-eki-e-ikimasu share the same English but each front carries a hint naming the particle, so both are answerable.
- が versus は: chapter-6/ga-particle has a long note but a useful one, and the pairs chapter-6-extras/sp2-kono-basu-wa-shibuya-ni-ikimasu-ka / ga-dono-basu-ga-kuukou-ni-ikimasu-ka and ga-kono-basu-wa-kuukou-ni-ikimasu-ka make the contrast concrete.
- Answer cards carry scenes that tell me what is being answered without giving it away: chapter-6-extras/a-iie-kimasen, a-iie-kimasen-deshita, quiz-kyonen-kimashita, quiz-hitori-de-kimashita, ga-goban-no-basu-ga-ikimasu, fib-sanban-no-basu-ikimasu, td-kato-san-to-ikimasu.
- chapter-6/fib-c6-osaka-branch-past-a (answer in the same tense as the question) and fib-c6-where-going-a (drop what both speakers know) have notes that teach something I would have got wrong.
- chapter-6/dewa: the "fuller, more formal form" hint keeps it apart from じゃ.

## Weak spots

**Production fronts with a second right answer**

- chapter-6/dono (should-fix): "Which (of three or more things)" against Lesson 5's どれ "Which one (of three or more things)". Only the note says どの goes before a noun.
- chapter-6-extras/ga-dore-ga-sumisu-san-no-kaban-desu-ka (should-fix): "Which one is Smith-san's bag?" is the Lesson 5 shape スミスさんのかばんはどれですか, and the card's note agrees that shape is correct.
- chapter-6-extras/ga-kore-ga-sumisu-san-no-desu (polish): near-identical English to Lesson 2's これはスミスさんのです.
- chapter-6-extras/td-dewa-ashita (polish): じゃ、あした fits "Well then, see you tomorrow." just as well.
- chapter-6-extras/to-koohii-to-koucha-o-onegaishimasu (polish): "Coffee and tea, please." is the deck's ください wording, not its おねがいします wording.

**Missing hint**

- chapter-6-extras/td-tokyo-shisha-no-sumisu (should-fix): without "on the phone", "This is Smith, of the Tokyo branch." reads as introducing someone, which I answer with こちらは…さんです.
- chapter-6/party-e-sentence, chapter-6-extras/he-ginza-no-depaato-e-ikimashita, he-kouen-e-ikimasu-ka (polish): へ sentences whose Production fronts never ask for へ, so I answer with に.

**Wrong or confusing wording (polish)**

- chapter-6/e-particle: the hint glosses に as "in/on/at", a meaning no card has taught; this lesson's に card says "To".
- chapter-6/douzo-kochira-e: English "Please come in." while the note says the こちら means "this way".
- chapter-6/sochira: the note says こちら has been taught only for people, not places, but the next card teaches こちら as "this way".
- chapter-6/tachi-example: the only たち example is ともだちたち, which doubles the "dachi" already in ともだち and reads doubtful.
- chapter-6-extras/td-hitori-de-kimasu-ka: the note's "her" and "his" belong to a dialogue I never see.

**Ordering (polish)**

- chapter-6/ks3-restaurant-friend uses きのう, whose card is excluded from the base unit and appears only in the extras.

**Notes that point at things I cannot see (polish, unit level)**

- Seven extras notes cite GRAMMAR 1, 2, 3, 5, WORD POWER or the EXERCISE I chart; chapter-6-extras/form-tokyo-shisha-kimasen-deshita's note is nothing but that citation.
- Nine extras notes number the card as "Member 1" to "Member 4" of a set, or "the first of four cards", which a shuffled deck never shows together.
