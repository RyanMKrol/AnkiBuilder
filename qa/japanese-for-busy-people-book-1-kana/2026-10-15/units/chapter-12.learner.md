# Lesson 12 (chapter-12 + chapter-12-extras): learner pass

This chapter was pleasant to study and mostly self-contained. It teaches あります and います, に for where something is, the position words (うえ, した, なか, まえ, うしろ, となり, ちかく, よこ), や for an open list, よ, 〜ってなんですか, and なにも/だれも with a negative. Almost every sentence is the same frame, "X の [position] に Y が あります/います", so once the base unit's word cards were in place I could build nearly every extras sentence from them. Short replies all carry a scene saying which question they answer, so I always knew what they wanted. The problems are few. The three 〜ってなんですか cards and the よ example never tell the Production front that the new form is wanted, so I would give the plain は/です version I already knew. One hint, repeated on five cards, says ちかく cannot take です, which clashes with the "X は [place] です" pattern the deck has drilled since Lesson 5. One authored card writes ラジャ in hiragana. The rest is polish: a reversed note, a stray "plain form" hint, a wrong lesson number, and a few romaji spellings that drift from the deck's macron style.

## Strong spots

- **あります vs います.** arimasu-inanimate and imasu-animate share the English "Be, exist" but each front carries a hint ("about a thing being in a place" vs "saying a person or animal is there"), so neither face collides. ex12-uketsuke-ni-tanaka-san-ga-imasu and f12-kinou-kaigishitsu-ni-tanaka-san-ga-imashita reinforce the split in their notes.
- **や vs と.** ya-particle-and, nikko-otera-jinja-arimasu and ikkai-ginkou-conveni-to put "and other sights" or "(and nothing else)" in the English, so the Production front tells me which particle to use. The extras answers (ex12-jinja-ya-otera-ga-arimasu, ex12-teemapaaku-ya-onsen-ga-arimasu, ex12-mizuumi-ya-onsen-ga-arimasu, ex12-ookii-otera-ya-jinja-ga-arimasu) keep the "and other sights" cue, and their scenes name the place.
- **Root and phrase pairs.** onna-female / onna-no-hito-woman and otoko-male / otoko-no-hito-man use hints ("the bare root word" vs "the full phrase") that keep both directions answerable.
- **Question and answer pairs with scenes.** ikkai-nani-ga-arimasuka / ginkou-ga-arimasu, hikidashi-naka-nani-arimasuka / nanimo-arimasen, tonari-no-heya-dare-imasuka / dare-mo-imasen, and the Goa set (fib-goa-desu, fib-goa-donna-tokoro-answer, fib-cashew-nuts-yuumei) all tell me which question the reply answers.
- **Helpful contrast notes.** ex12-nikkou-ni-mizuumi-ga-arimasu (みずうみ vs うみ), ex12-nikkou-ni-jinja-ga-arimasu (shrine vs temple), ex12-ryokan-no-tonari-ni-nani-ga-arimasu-ka (となり vs ちかく), ex12-sangai-ni-dare-ga-imasu-ka and ex12-yonkai-ni-nani-ga-arimasu-ka (floor readings), fib-resutoran-ni-mo-mizu-ga-arimasu (the hint says which noun is the "also").

## Weak spots

**missing-info (should-fix)**

- onsen-tte-nandesuka, fib-teemapaaku-tte-nan-desu-ka, p12-tenpura-tte-nan-desu-ka: the Production front is just "What is an onsen?" / "What is a theme park?" / "What is tempura?". From the ledger I would say おんせんはなんですか, the これはなんですか shape. Nothing on the front says I am echoing a word I just heard.
- nihon-no-supa-desu-yo: the Production English "It's a Japanese spa." gives no cue for よ. Earlier the deck put よ as "you know" (きれいなところですよ, "It's a pretty place, you know."). Here I would leave it out.

**note-unverifiable (should-fix)**

- chikaku-near: the hint says ちかく "is followed by に (ni) and あります (arimasu), not です (desu)". The same hint is on ryokan-chikaku-nani-arimasuka, fib-daigaku-no-chikaku-ni-ginkou-ya-yuubinkyoku-ga-arimasu, p12-eki-no-chikaku-ni-yuubinkyoku-mo-arimasu and v12-sofaa-no-chikaku-ni-terebi-ga-arimasu. The deck taught me "X は [place] です" (インフォメーションは１かいです, おてあらいはあちらです), so "X は えきの ちかくです" ought to work, and the hint tells me it does not.

**wrong-content**

- fib-raja-uchi-indo-doko-desuka (should-fix): writes らじゃさん in hiragana. Every earlier card writes ラジャ in katakana.
- ookii-mizuumi-ya-taki (polish): "There are a big lake, waterfall, and other things." reads badly in English.
- fib-cashew-nuts-yuumei, fib-nani-ga-yuumei-desuka, fib-teemapaaku-tte-nan-desu-ka (polish): romaji "kashuunattsu", "yuumei", "teemapaaku" where the word cards say "kashū nattsu" and "tēma pāku" and the deck uses macrons.

**other (polish)**

- dare-mo-imasen: the note has the swap backwards ("with なに (nani) in place of だれ (dare)").
- sobaya-soba-shop: a "plain form" hint on a noun. I was never taught what plain form means, and a noun has no other form here.
- f12-kyonen-eki-no-mae-ni-konbini-ga-arimashita: the note says いきました is from Lesson 8. I learned it in Lesson 6.

**note-unverifiable (polish)**

- ex12-kaigishitsu-ni-dare-ga-imasu-ka: "takes に (ni) directly with no の (no) in front, because it is already a place noun rather than a position word". I could not work out what の it means.
- eg-chairoi-tsukue: "ちゃいろ (chairo) is the other both-ways colour". The note never says which colour the first one is.

**ambiguous (polish)**

- side: the よこ note glosses いすの よこ as "next to the chair", and となり (tonari-next-to) is the card whose Production front is "Next to".
