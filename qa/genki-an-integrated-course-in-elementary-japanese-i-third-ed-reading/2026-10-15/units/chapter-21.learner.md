# Chapter 22 (Reading and Writing 8: Japanese Office Workers): Learner

This was a comfortable chapter to study. Seven new single kanji (回, 葉, 鮮, 不, 品, 池, 駐) each turn up again in the words straight after them: 新鮮な, 不思議な, 作品, 電池, 駐車場, 次回, 言葉. Most of the other words are built from kanji I already knew (電, 話, 作, 員, 次, 読, 思, 返, 残, 車). So nearly every card could be worked out from its parts and then confirmed on the back. Every word card has its romaji, and none of the English collides with a word I learned earlier on a front I could confuse with it.

## Strong spots

- The new kanji come with the words that use them: 鮮 (r-8a8d66c36e9f) with 新鮮な (r-5b46105fd111), 池 (r-33dda9d445d7) with 電池 (r-9fe842efdc6a), 駐 (r-7cf079ed8635) with 駐車場 (r-53ba1f65fb7b), 不 (r-8fa7401e9077) with 不思議な (r-34b3a73d2e06).
- Words that pay off earlier kanji: 会員 (r-fdaff61a3b22), 駅員 (r-013dd9aa0c97) and 店員 (r-afd029cc4d02) all use 員 "Member"; 次回 (r-57737bc0fa9a) and 次女 (r-76c0aabc1119) use 次; 次女 sits well next to 長女 from Reading and Writing 5.
- The pairs 作者 / 作家 and 言葉 / 単語 have different fronts and distinct enough English that they never get in each other's way.
- 駐 plays its one reading like every other single-reading kanji in the deck, so it is consistent with what I am used to.
