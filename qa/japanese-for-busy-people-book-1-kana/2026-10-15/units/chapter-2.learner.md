# Lesson 2: Possession: Whose Pen Is This? (learner pass)

Units: `chapter-2` (46 cards), then `chapter-2-extras` (74 cards).

This chapter was easy to study. It builds on Lesson 1 one piece at a time: これ, then の for
possession, then じゃありません, then だれの, and the extras turn those into many short sentences
I could say without help. Almost every short answer ("No, it isn't mine.", "It's Sasaki-san's.")
has a scene telling me what question it answers, so I never had to guess which of several near
twins was wanted. The digits arrive in time for the two phone-number cards, and those cards'
notes explain how to read a number aloud. The problems are small and they cluster in one place:
a few Production fronts say "your" in English when the Japanese avoids "you", and I had already
learned あなた in Lesson 1, so I kept putting it in. Two notes also disagree about how polite
わかりません is.

## Strong spots

- Scenes on short answers are consistent and do their job: `iie-kasa-ja-arimasen`,
  `ema-san-no-desu`, `sasaki-san-no-desu`, `iie-watashi-no-desu`, `nihon-no-okashi-desu`,
  `nozomi-depaato-desu`.
- Minimal pairs that are taught as pairs: `kore-wa-kagi-desu-ka` (only か changes, no word
  reordering), `megane-wa-watashi-no-ja-arimasen`, `kaban-wa-sasaki-san-no-desu` (the topic choice
  explained).
- Homophones handled well: `num-3` explains さん the number against さん the title, and `watch`
  sets うでどけい apart from とけい with a clear hint.
- Notes that add something: `kore-wa-watashi-no-denwa-ja-arimasen` (でんわ the object vs
  でんわばんごう the information), `sumisu-san-no-desu-ka` (why あなた is avoided),
  `sumisu-san-no-denwa-bangou-wa` and `nozomi-depaato-no-denwa-bangou` (の for each hyphen),
  `iie-watashi-no-desu` (naming the owner is a denial by itself).

**note-unverifiable**

  `saa-wakarimasen` says the bare form "can land abruptly".

**ambiguous**

- `chapter-2/kore` (polish): "This one" on its own also fits こちら from Lesson 1.

**unanswerable**

- `chapter-2-extras/fib-kagi-wa-dare-no-desu-ka` (polish): "Whose key is it?" does not signal

**untaught-prerequisite**

**other**

- `chapter-2-extras/namae-o-oshiete-kudasai` (should-fix): the note says you would normally say
  おなまえ when asking someone's name, which is exactly what this card asks for.

  particle, prefix or counter that has not been taught yet.
- `chapter-2-extras/hai-sasaki-san-no-desu` (polish): the note cites "GRAMMAR 3" from a book I
  don't have.
- `chapter-2-extras/kore-wa-sasaki-san-no-saifu-desu-ka` (polish): the note says "answers below",
  which means nothing in a shuffled deck.
