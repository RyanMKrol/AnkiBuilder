**Grammar and notes (13)**

1. Two kinds of adjective; an い-adjective goes straight before a noun (おおきい こうえん).
2. A な-adjective takes な before a noun (きれいな はな).
3. い-adjective predicate: 〜いです / 〜くないです.
4. Exception: いいです / よくないです.
5. な-adjective predicate: 〜です / 〜じゃありません.
6. 〜ではありません as the more formal negative (grammar table, marked "more formal").
7. NOTES 1: Thing を どうぞ, offering.
8. NOTES 2: Thing は いかがですか, polite offer.
9. NOTES 3: はい、いただきます, accepting with thanks.
10. Speaking NOTES: おじゃまします, said entering private space as a guest.
11. Exercise IX: X は どんな ところですか / Adj ところです.
12. Exercise X: praising with Adj + noun + ですね.
13. Exercise XI: weather small talk, ええ、ほんとうに 〜ですね.

**Tables to memorise (57 cells)**: Exercise I (14 い-adjectives × present aff., present neg.,

**Sentences (17)**: the dialogue's eight lines including the summary sentence, Key Sentences 1 to 4,

## Findings

**book-gap**

- `-` (chapter-10): no card's back says that an い-adjective goes straight before a noun with no な,
  the half of GRAMMAR 1 the な-adjective notes contrast against (should-fix).
- `-` (chapter-10): いいです, the affirmative cell of いい in the GRAMMAR table and Exercise I, has no
  card (polish).
- `-` (chapter-10): 〜ではありません, the "more formal" negative in the GRAMMAR table, appears only
  inside `e-yuumei-a`'s note (polish).

**wrong-content**

- `machimasu`: note calls まちます the "plain/dictionary form"; it is the polite ます form (blocker).
- `matte-kudasai`: note sends the learner to `machimasu` "for the plain dictionary form" (should-fix).

**book-mismatch**

- `itadakimasu-accept`: English starts "Yes, thank you" but the target drops the book's はい
  (polish).
- `ojama-shimasu`: English "Sorry to intrude." where the book glosses "May I come in?" (polish).

**other**

- `totemo`: note cites a card "What a very old clock!" that does not exist (the card is "It's a very
  old clock, isn't it?") and says ほんとうに is "below" (polish).
- `hontouni`: note cites "Yeah, it's really cold." but the card reads "Yeah, it really is cold,
  isn't it?" (polish).
- `pretty` (extras): a second card for きれい(な) in the same chapter as base `kirei` (polish).
