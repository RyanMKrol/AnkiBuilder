## Findings

**untaught-prerequisite**

- `haha-ga-kureru-okashi-ga-suki` (should-fix): ははがくれるおかし uses a verb clause to modify a noun.
  The chapter puts the dictionary form only before の.
- `niau-fuku-wo-kau-no-ga-suki` (should-fix): にあうふく, the same untaught noun-modifying clause.
- `nihongo-no-anime-ga-wakaru-no-wa-tanoshii` (polish): uses の + は + たのしいです. The chapter
  teaches の only before が and も.

**wrong-content**

- `haha-ga-kureru-okashi-ga-suki` (should-fix): the note says "A verb describing a noun is always in
  the dictionary form", which is false.
- `kiku-listen-dict-form` (polish): the note implies きく is the dictionary form of the "listen" sense
  only.

**book-mismatch**

- `fib-chan-robotto-anime-suki-a` (polish): the English says "(he)", but the book's Chan is a woman,
  and the same unit's Chan cards say "She".
- `x15a-itoko-no-pooru` (polish): the note calls いとこの's の "the same の as にほんのアニメ". Lesson 6
  says the apposition の is "not possession or affiliation".

**other**

- `amazing` (polish): an absorbed course card gives すごい a second card in this chapter ("Amazing!")
  alongside the book's すごい! "That's great!".
