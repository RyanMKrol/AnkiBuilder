  not, and the extras card vocab-rainen-amerika-ni-ikimasu says so. senshuu's "but not the year

  as plural). tachi-suffix's note and hitori-de-kouen-ni-ikimashita's note use the same form. The
  book's example is ひとたち.

**book-mismatch**

- chapter-6/e-particle (should-fix): the Production hint glosses に as "in/on/at". The book glosses
  に here as "to", and so does this unit's own に card.

**untaught-prerequisite**

- chapter-6/ga-particle (polish): the scene uses a だれが question. The book teaches が after どれ and
  どの, and no card teaches だれが.

**note-unverifiable**

- chapter-6-extras/hai-hitori-de-ikimasu (polish): "a bare はい would sound abrupt". The book
  presents short answers as normal.
- chapter-6-extras/vocab-tokyo-eki-ni-ikimasu-ka (polish): "never とうきょうのえき". That phrase is
  valid Japanese with a different meaning.
