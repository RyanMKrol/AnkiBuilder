# Lesson 15 (chapter-15 + chapter-15-extras): learner pass

This chapter was heavy but fair to study. It brings in すき and じょうず with が, a batch of hobby nouns, and the dictionary form. The dictionary form is the real work. The base unit gives me a dictionary-form card for almost every verb I already know, each tagged "(dictionary form)" on the Production front, and each note says which ます form it comes from and what sound changes. The extras then use those forms in のがすきです sentences built from words I already have (ともだちにあう, しゃしんをみせる, ふじさんにのぼる). Nearly every sentence could be answered from the ledger plus this lesson's word cards. The trouble is mostly in the notes and in a few fronts. Notes keep sorting verbs into "Regular 1" and "Regular 2" without ever telling me how to tell the two apart. One note mixes up two kinds of の that the deck taught me to keep apart. Two Production fronts want a dictionary form without the tag that every other such card has. A handful of glosses ("But", "Same here", "Japanese food", "Korean food") also fit words I learned earlier.

## Strong spots

- **Dictionary-form drill.** iku-dict-form, kau-dict-form, nomu-dict-form, kasu-dict-form, matsu-dict-form, oshieru-dict-form, kuru-dict-form, suru-dict-form and the rest each name the source ます form and the lesson I learned it in, so I can hang the new form on something I know. Every Production front carries "(dictionary form)", so 'Swim' versus 'Swim (dictionary form)' (oyogimasu-swim, oyogu-dict-form) is never in doubt.
- **The trap verbs are flagged.** kaeru-dict-form, toru-dict-form, wakaru-dict-form and kariru-dict-form each warn about the る ending, and kariru-dict-form says outright "Not かる".
- **The nominalizer の.** no-nominalizer separates it from the possessive and apposition の, and x15a-e-o-kaku-no-ga-suki-q explains the を inside the clause next to the が outside it.
- **も versus が.** x15b-utau-no-ga-suki and x15a-kaku-no-mo-suki explain which particle to use, and ex7-eg-kato-me-too-sing has a scene that tells me why も is right.
- **Thing versus action.** x15c-wain-nomu-no-ga-suki ("likes the WINE" versus "likes the drinking of it"), x15r-chan-beer-uta and x15r-sasaki-wine-sukii (noun じょうず versus verb のがじょうず) answer the questions I would have asked.
- **Reply scenes.** x15a-jazu-ga-suki, x15a-yakyuu-ga-suki and fib-chan-robotto-anime-suki-a each say which question they answer.
- **New patterns on the extras.** haha-ga-kureru-okashi-ga-suki and niau-fuku-wo-kau-no-ga-suki explain a verb in front of a noun with words I already know, and their notes make the new pattern clear.
