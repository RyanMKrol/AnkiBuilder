## Findings

**wrong-content (5)**

- kirei-deshita, taihen-na, tanoshii (should-fix): the notes call the polite です-style past
  (きれいでした, たいへんでした, たのしかったです) the "plain past form". The book's table is headed
  "As predicate: adjective + です"; "plain" names the だ/casual forms the learner meets later. The
  same mislabel appears outside this chapter (chapter-10/matte-kudasai calls まちます "the plain
  dictionary form"), so it looks like a pattern.
- arukimasu (should-fix): the note calls あるきます the "dictionary/plain form"; it is the ます form.
- ii-desu (polish): says all of いい's other forms are built from よく; よかった is not, and the
  sibling yokatta-desu-good says the stem is よ.

**book-gap (1)**

- dono-kurai (polish): NOTES 1's どのぐらい variant is on no card.

**note-unverifiable (2)**

- dou-itashimashite (polish): the Production-front hint calls the phrase stiff and says people
  usually brush thanks aside; the book gives it plainly as the host's reply.
- ex11-sumisu-san-kinou-wa-omiyage-o-arigatou-gozaimashita (polish): a long note about
  きのうのおみやげ that the book does not support.

production length, note-claim flags, and the omatsuri/konsaato corpus-mirror drift).
