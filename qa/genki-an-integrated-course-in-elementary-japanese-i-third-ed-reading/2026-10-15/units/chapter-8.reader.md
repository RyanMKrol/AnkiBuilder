## Faithful

The deck carries this chapter well. Every kanji word is spelled as the book prints it and carries the
book's kana reading (眼鏡 めがね, 兄弟 きょうだい, 四人 よにん, 九人 きゅうにん, 一人 ひとり, 二人 ふたり). The
English follows the book's glosses closely ("Cafeteria; dining commons", "To put on (clothes above
your waist)", "Not…anything (with a negative)", "Nothing in particular"), and the kinship cards
carry the table's who-is-speaking distinctions ("My husband (formal)", "Someone else's husband",
"My grandfather (formal)") so the many family words stay apart. The body-part labels in the
pictures were all picked up. The phrase and inflected-form exclusions follow the words-only ruling,
and no card is a proper name, a sentence or an AI-written addition.

Things I checked and left alone:

- Character and word pairs that share an English (男/男の人 "Man", 女/女の人 "Woman", 眉/眉毛
  "Eyebrow", 働/働く "To work", 速/速い "Fast"). The chapter-4 and chapter-7 readers left the same
  pattern alone as outside rule 9's examples, and it is the learner's question rather than the
  book's.
- 背 is a silent character card ("Back; height") rather than a voiced word せ, although the
  exclusion of 背が高い/背が低い names it as one of "its words". Its readings line includes "se" and
  its English covers the sense, so I did not report it.
- 眼 "Eye (medical)" comes from the kanji dictionary, not the book, and is worded to stay apart
  from 目 "Eye".
