# Chapter 09: Lesson 7: Family Picture (chapter-8), Learner

This was a heavy chapter: 47 new single kanji, most of them body parts and family members, then 52
words built from them. It was still easy to study. Every front is just the written form, every back
answers the question cleanly, and the words almost always reuse kanji I had just met (家 plus 族 for
家族, 便 from Lesson 4 plus the new 利 for 便利, 会 plus 社 for 会社). The irregular readings that I
could not have worked out from the kanji (一人 hitori, 二人 futari, 四人 yo-nin, 眼鏡 megane, 眉毛 mayuge)
are each taught on their own back, which is where I needed them. I found four small gloss problems
and nothing that stops a card from being studied.

## Strong spots

- The family set mostly makes the "my family" versus "someone else's family" split visible on the
  back: 兄 / 姉 / 父 / 母 say "(My) ...", 弟さん, 妹さん, お子さん, 奥さん and ご主人 say "Someone
  else's ...", and 家内 / 主人 are marked "(formal)" (r-1b60f62fba6e, r-b1a3afebdb30, r-0b27d61a0761,
  r-78ce0b021c44, r-cd29568063bd, r-a9976079705d, r-339883a9e19c, r-8dfa494661bc, r-346413644cb8,
  r-f7c743bbd4c0, r-636e7702bc4b).
- Near neighbours are separated by the English: 髪 "Hair (on the head)" against 毛 "Fur; body hair"
  (r-f45938c905d5, r-f749203bd901); 眼 "Eye (medical)" against the Lesson 6 目 "Eye" (r-6191d800ec61);
  速い "Fast" against the Lesson 3 早い "Early" (r-8586dc33c850).
- 着る says "clothes above your waist" (r-94959c02dce1) and 低い adds "short (in height)"
  (r-c022ef791faa), which is exactly the detail a learner would otherwise get wrong.
- 何も flags that it needs a negative (r-3f9f4a086bc1).
- The ten people-counter cards (五人, 七人, 八人 and the rest) each give their reading, so the
  irregular ones never have to be guessed.

## Weak spots

**missing-info (4, all polish)**

- 弟 (r-276330ed9702) and 妹 (r-28f894973975) say "Younger brother" and "Younger sister" with no
  "(My)", unlike 兄 and 姉 in the same set. The back stops teaching that the plain form is the one for
  your own family.
- うちの人 (r-ffb5060bdee1) says "Husband (informal)" while うちの子 says "My child (informal)" and
  主人 says "My husband (formal)". Whose husband is not stated.
- 別に (r-715a30074c48) says "Nothing in particular" with no mention that it goes with a negative,
  which 何も does mention. Fine as a one-word reply, but no help in a sentence.

Not reported, because preflight already lists it: the romaji style note on 着 (r-cceb350d8ed5).
