**Grammar and notes (6)**

1. person 1 は person 2 に noun を あげます; に marks the receiver (GRAMMAR 1, KS1).
2. あげます cannot be used for giving to the speaker; くれます instead (GRAMMAR 1 NOTE).
3. person 1 は person 2 に noun を もらいます; に here means "from", the giver (GRAMMAR 2, KS2).
4. person は noun が あります = "have"; the thing had takes が (GRAMMAR 3, KS3).
5. A count follows "noun が" with no particle: かいぎが ２つ あります (GRAMMAR 3 e.g.).
6. NOTES 1 いいんですか to confirm an unexpected offer; NOTES 2 clause + から giving a reason.

**Table (20 cells)**: EXERCISES I, "memorize their forms": あげます, もらいます, あります, かします,
かります, each in present aff./neg. and past aff./neg.

**Sentences (35)**: KEY SENTENCES 1-3; the GRAMMAR 3 example; eight dialogue lines including the

## Findings

**book-mismatch**

- `chapter-14/mafuraa` (should-fix): the note says マフラー and スカーフ "both mean the same thing".
  The book glosses マフラー "muffler, scarf" and draws it as a man's knitted winter scarf, distinct
  from the woman's decorative スカーフ in the same picture.
- `chapter-14/kara-particle-because` (polish): the note calls から a "Suffix". The book and the
  card's own English call it a particle.
- `chapter-14/yoku` (polish): the scene cue reads "about how skillfully". The book teaches よく here
  only as the "well" in よく にあいますね, which the card's own note also says.

**wrong-content**

- `chapter-14-extras/eg-kuroi-beruto` (should-fix): the note says くろい, あかい, あおい, しろい are
  the colour い-adjectives "and no others". The deck already teaches きいろい and ちゃいろい as
  い-adjectives in earlier units.
- `chapter-14-extras/fib-tanjoubi-ni-kuremasu` (polish): the scene says it answers about
  "Smith-san's father gives him money", but たんじょうびに くれます and "gives it to me" need the
  speaker to be the receiver. Its paired question `fib-chichi-itsu-okane-kuremasu-q` asks about the
  asker's own father (ちち) giving the asker (わたしに) money. The two halves do not describe the
  same exchange.

No untaught-prerequisite, proper-name or schematic-pattern findings. None of the above repeats
preflight's output for these units (romaji style, answerable-alone, production length, the
note-claim flags, and the three corpus-mirror romaji notes on `arimasu-have`, `kyouto-omiyage` and
`mafuraa`).
