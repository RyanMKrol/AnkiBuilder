# Chapter 08: Lesson 6: A Day in Robert's Life (chapter-7), learner spot-check

This chapter is a steady one to study. Most of it is kanji: about fifty single characters, then the
verbs and school words built from them. Because every kanji in the original words is carded before
the words that use it, each compound reads as a new combination of pieces I already know (幼 + 稚 +
園, 短 + 期 + 大学, 教 + 科 + 書). The verbs pair cleanly with their kanji cards (開 and 開ける, 閉
and 閉める, 消 and 消す), and the kanji romaji lines list the reading each verb uses. The seven
additions from the fix pass (一つ目, 二つ目, 義務教育, 夏休み, 冬休み, 春休み, 寮) study fine on
their own backs. Three of them, though, use kanji that no card has taught alone, which this deck has
otherwise never done.

## Strong spots

- The school-level set reads as a system: 保育園 (r-ff9f892a080a), 幼稚園 (r-132fa45a7a57), 小学校
  (r-327cdf816423), 中学校 (r-2e5fecd2cc7f), 高等学校 (r-4d2e8f09ab66), 短期大学
  (r-365a0d35af38), 大学院 (r-6293f3d771e4). Every kanji in them is carded.
- Kanji readings point at the words: 降 "kō / o, fu" for 降りる (r-5edea29b72cf), 側 "soku / gawa"
  for 左側 (r-d9bf8247a0c9), 伝 "den / tsuta, tsuda, tsute" for 手伝う (r-17b40d6d30ec).
- 一つ目 (r-edb065c8a1a2) and 二つ目 (r-503adf9ba77c) fill the gap the words-only cut left in the
  direction phrases, and both are built from kanji I know (一, 二, 目).
- 寮 (r-7cb3b66a1f4c) matches the kana card りょう "Dormitory" I already had.
- 春休み (r-e266c482e39b) is fully built from known kanji (春, 休み).

## Weak spots

**other** (polish): a kanji with no card of its own.

- 夏休み (r-a5e487dc05dc): 夏 has no card.
- 冬休み (r-1d773007ebc9): 冬 has no card.
- 義務教育 (r-7a4fe7758104): 義 and 務 have no card, though 教 and 育 do.

Each card still works, since its back teaches the whole word. But they are the only words so far
whose kanji I meet for the first time inside a compound.
