# Chapter 0, Frequently Used Expressions: the Learner

This is the first unit, so the ledger is empty and every card has to teach itself. It mostly does.
The eighteen set phrases are short, the backs give a clear English meaning plus a note on when to
say it, and the notes point at each other (morning, afternoon, evening; leaving early and the reply
to it; before and after a meal) so the chapter hangs together as a small system rather than a list.
The hard part of the unit is the cluster of four "Good-bye." cards and the two "Excuse me." cards,
and the scenes and hints handle that cluster well on the Production side. The one place it slips is
a Recognition front for しつれいします (shitsurei shimasu) whose scene does not say which of the
two meanings it wants. Everything else is small.

## Strong spots

- **The time-of-day greetings** (`ohayou-gozaimasu`, `konnichiwa`, `konbanwa`): the notes give
  matching boundaries (before about 10:00, 10:00 to sundown, after dark), so the three cards teach
  one rule from three directions.
- **The Good-bye cluster on Production** (`sayounara`, `shitsurei-shimasu-leaving`,
  `osaki-ni-shitsurei-shimasu`, `otsukaresama-deshita`): four fronts read "Good-bye." and each one's
  scene or hint picks a single answer. The pairing notes on `osaki-ni-shitsurei-shimasu` and
  `otsukaresama-deshita` (one leaves, the other replies) are the best notes in the unit.
- **Excuse me, two ways** (`shitsurei-shimasu-entering`, `sumimasen-excuse-me`): the entering card's
  hint says outright "not for getting a stranger's attention", which is exactly the line between
  them.
- **すみません (sumimasen) as two cards** (`sumimasen-excuse-me`, `sumimasen-sorry`): the scenes
  decide the Recognition face cleanly and each note points at the other.
- **The two request phrases** (`chotto-matte-kudasai`, `mou-ichido-onegaishimasu`): the notes break
  each phrase into its parts, so the learner leaves knowing ちょっと (chotto), まつ (matsu), ください
  (kudasai), もういちど (mou ichido) and おねがいします (onegaishimasu) as pieces, not just as two
  sentences.
- **ごちそうさま（でした） (gochisōsama (deshita))** works with no scene at all, and its note explains
  the optional でした (deshita).

## Weak spots

**ambiguous**

- `shitsurei-shimasu-leaving` (Recognition, should-fix): the front is "しつれいします / said on
  formal occasions". Walking into a boss's office is also a formal occasion, which is the other
  しつれいします card (`shitsurei-shimasu-entering`, "Excuse me."). The front never says leaving,
  so I cannot tell "Good-bye." from "Excuse me." until the back.

**leaks-answer**

- `itadakimasu` (Recognition, should-fix): the scene "about to start eating" is the answer "(Said
  before eating a meal)" in other words, so the Recognition face asks nothing. There is only one
  いただきます (itadakimasu) card, and its sibling `gochisousama-deshita` works with no scene.
- Borderline, not filed: `douzo` ("offering something to someone") and `iie-not-at-all`
  ("downplaying thanks just received") also lean towards the answer on Recognition, but their scenes
  are doing real work on Production ("Please." and "Not at all." are loose English), so they stay.

**missing-info**

- `iie-not-at-all` (polish): the note ends "not a direct answer to a yes/no question". A learner who
  has never met いいえ (iie) before can read that as "いいえ never answers a yes/no question", when
  the word's plain meaning is "no".
- `arigatou-gozaimasu` (polish): the hint calls this "the polite full-length form", which implies a
  shorter form, but no card or note shows it.
- `osaki-ni-shitsurei-shimasu` (polish): the note explains when to say it but not what おさきに
  (osaki ni) adds to しつれいします (shitsurei shimasu), which the learner already knows from two
  other cards.

**untaught-prerequisite**

- `chotto-matte-kudasai` (polish): the note says "まって is the て-form of まつ" and the
  `mou-ichido-onegaishimasu` note repeats "a verb's て-form". Nothing has said what a て-form is.

**other**

- `gochisousama-deshita` (polish): the romaji is "gochisōsama (deshita)." on the card but
  "gochisousama" in its own note and in the `itadakimasu` note. Preflight's inline romaji check
  catches the same mismatch on three other cards in this unit but not this one.
