# Chapter 10: Lesson 8: Barbecue (chapter-9), Learner

This was a pleasant chapter to study. There are 43 single kanji first and then 39 words. Almost every
word is built from a kanji I had just met, or from one I already knew: 牛 plus 肉 for 牛肉, 天気 from
Lesson 5 plus the new 予 and 報 for 天気予報, 会社 from Lesson 7 plus 員 for 会社員. The kanji that
have only one reading (料 ryō, 理 ri, 員 in, 予 yo, 濯 taku) are voiced, and the ones with several
are silent, the same as in earlier chapters, so I knew how to grade each one. The single-kanji words
(冬, 夏, 雪, 雨, 肉, 所, 度) give the word reading and the word's meaning. The readings I could not have
worked out from the kanji cards (今朝 kesa, 上手 jōzu, 下手 heta) are taught on their own backs. I
found nothing worth a finding.

## Strong spots

- Near neighbours are kept apart by the English. 鶏 "Chicken" sits beside 鶏肉 "Chicken (meat)"
  (r-0604876a6817, r-c3c8e08c555f). 事 "Thing (abstract); matter" sits against the Lesson 4 物
  "Thing (physical)" (r-aae488416289). 気温 "Temperature (weather)" sits against 度 "Degrees
  (temperature)" (r-7aeef663f3d7, r-304fce687a69).
- 持っていく "To take (a thing)" (r-3177ccc0660e) pairs cleanly with the Lesson 6 持ってくる "To bring
  (a thing)", so the direction is clear from the English.
- 降る "To fall (rain/snow)" (r-5abfcbebeed7) says what falls, so it cannot be confused with the
  Lesson 6 降りる "To get off".
- The weather set reads as one group: 晴れ, 曇り, 雨, 雪, 天気予報, 気温 (r-0324f8ecbb96,
  r-283112eb7d8b, r-8995b8979971, r-7d742a7f9f36, r-97d2e2825721, r-7aeef663f3d7).
- 始める "To begin (something)" (r-66901a639dd6) marks that it takes an object.
- The suru verbs (掃除する, 洗濯する, 運転する, 料理する) each reuse two kanji carded in this same
  chapter, so the kanji cards are put to use right away.

## Weak spots

None. Every front is the written form alone, every word's kanji has been carded by the time I meet
the word, and every back answered what the front asked.
