## Faithful

All 58 word cards spell the target exactly as the book does (〜代 is carded as 代), and every reading
matches the book's kana or ruby, including めいしゃ, ふつかよい, にさんにち and むしば. Every English gloss
says what the book says. Where the deck changed a gloss, it did so to keep two answers apart under
rule 9: 歯科 "Dentistry (dental clinic)" beside 歯医者 "Dentist", and 眼科 "Ophthalmology (eye
clinic)" beside 目医者 "Eye doctor". 卵, 服, 彼 and 代 are taught as words, so they are correctly voiced
word cards rather than silent kanji cards. The voiced KANJIDIC cards (婦, 症, 膚, 績, 疹, 緊, 抗, 痢, 邪)
are the kanji with a single reading, as designed. Every kanji in a shipping word has a card in this
unit or an earlier one. All 18 exclusions have sound words-only reasons. No card is aiSuggested, and
no card teaches a proper name.

I left two things alone. First, 疹 "Rash" with 発疹 "Rash", and 痢 "Diarrhea" with 下痢 "Diarrhea",
share an English answer. But by design, preflight compares kanji cards only with other kanji cards,
and these are the characters' own dictionary meanings. Second, 代's romaji "~dai" keeps the book's
〜 placeholder. 時間 "~jikan" in chapter-5 does the same, so this is a collection convention and not
a fidelity issue.
