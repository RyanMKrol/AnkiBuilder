# Lesson 8 (chapter-8 + chapter-8-extras): learner pass

This was a good chapter to study. It brings in を, the place sense of で, the four forms of the verbs (ます, ません, ました, ませんでした) and the noun + をします pattern, then drills them with a lot of short sentences built only from words I already had: Smith, Suzuki, Emma and Sasaki eating, drinking, buying, reading and watching things I know the names of. Almost every sentence card could be answered from the ledger plus this lesson's word cards. The scene hints on short replies ("answering where you study", "answering how many are in your party") do their job, and the pairs that could have collided (bare count vs. reply, place で vs. means で, に vs. で) are mostly kept apart. The trouble is in a handful of word cards: the なに card gives its answer away and its note gets なん wrong, two "how many people" fronts can't be told apart, and two weekend questions read the same in English.

## Strong spots

- **Particle contrasts in the notes.** p-resutoran-de-tabemasu vs p-resutoran-ni-ikimasu (に goes to the place, で does something there), p-kaado-de-kasa-kaimashita vs p-depaato-de-kasa-kaimashita (means vs place), p-suzuki-mo-kabuki-mimasu vs p-raamen-mo-tabemashita (も on the person vs the object). Each note adds something the card itself doesn't say.
- **Bare count vs. reply.** sannin / fib-sannin-desu, hitori / v-hitori-desu, futari / ex-futari-desu: the hints "the bare count" and "answering how many are in your party" separate every pair cleanly.
- **Register.** nanmeisama-desu-ka, ex-futari-desu and the でございます cards (degozaimasu, tenmasa-degozaimasu, nozomi-depaato-degozaimasu) explain who says what to whom.
- **Dropped objects.** ex-resutoran-de-tabemasu, v-toshokan-de-shimasu and v-hakone-de-shimashita teach that a reply leaves out what the question supplied, and their hints make the short answer guessable.
- **Conjugation drills** (f-kaimasu-arm-1..4, f-kikimasu-rajio-1..4, f-mimasu-eiga-1..4, f-yomimasu-nyuusu-1..4) are unambiguous from the English, and their tense is always clear.
- **Compound words with a note**: asagohan / hirugohan / bangohan, p102-eigakan-ni-ikimashita (えいが + かん, which ties back to としょかん and たいしかん), p102-senshuumatsu-nani-o-shimashita-ka.

## Weak spots

**leaks-answer**

- chapter-8/nani (should-fix): the Production hint prints なにをしますか (nani o shimasu ka) on the front that asks for なに.

**wrong-content**

- chapter-8/nani (should-fix): the note says なんじ and なんにん come from なんの 'what kind of'. Every card I studied, including this unit's nannin note, builds them from なん + a suffix.

**ambiguous**

- chapter-8/nannin vs chapter-8-extras/v-nannin-desu-ka (should-fix): "How many people / the plain, everyday way to ask" vs "How many people? / the everyday wording…" want なんにん and なんにんですか.
- chapter-8/fib-shuumatsu-nani-shimashitaka vs chapter-8-extras/p102-senshuumatsu-nani-o-shimashita-ka (should-fix): "What did you do over the weekend?" vs "What did you do last weekend?" mean the same thing in English.
- chapter-8/wakarimashita (polish): the gloss "Understood, I see, I understand" ends in the present tense, which belongs to わかります, and "I see" is already そうですか.

**missing-info**

- chapter-8/onamae, chapter-8/odenwabangou (polish): "Your name" and "Your phone number" fit なまえ / でんわばんごう or あなたの…; nothing asks for the honorific お.
- chapter-8-extras/p102-nichiyoubi-kaji-o-shimasu (polish): かじ (housework) is new and gets no note.

**other**

- chapter-8/kikimasu (polish): the hint "putting on music or a recording" suggests playing music, not listening.
- chapter-8/onamae and chapter-8-extras/v-asagohan-tabemasen (polish): notes say "below" and "directly above", which mean nothing in a shuffled deck.

Preflight already covers the ii-desu-ne answer-shaped English, the long tenpura Production face, the honorific-hyphen romaji, the uncarded forms named in the nanmeisama, f-kaimasu-arm-3 and たべもの notes, and the standalone-word list, so I have not repeated any of those.
