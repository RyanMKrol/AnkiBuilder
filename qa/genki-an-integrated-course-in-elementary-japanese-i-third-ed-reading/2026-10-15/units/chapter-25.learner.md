# Chapter 26 (Reading and Writing 12: Tanabata Festival): Learner

A pleasant last chapter. The twelve new single kanji (娘, 昔, 向, 景, 態, 織, 泣, 差, 々, 怒, 空, 畑) almost all come back straight away inside a word: 昔話, 昔々, 大昔, 向こう, 景色, 態度, 織る, 泣く, 差別, 怒る, 青空. The rest of the words lean on kanji I already had (度, 使, 起, 特, 別, 赤, 青, 早, 連, 働), so I could usually work a card out from its parts and let the back confirm it. The repetition mark 々 gets its own card and then turns up in 色々な, 別々に, 人々 and 昔々, which ties it to 時々 and 少々 from earlier chapters. Readings that break the pattern I would guess (人々 hitobito, 天の川 amanogawa, 子牛 kōshi, 特色 tokushoku) are all spelled out on the back. Only two small things caught me.

## Strong spots

- New kanji arrive with the words that use them: 昔 (r-cccfb185278a) with 昔話 (r-9784828eb92f), 大昔 (r-0e4f2a55b516) and 昔々 (r-0e0a3ab3a9b1); 向 (r-6dbcceb7f006) with 向こう (r-d948019761d3); 景 (r-f4f980e1e9fa) with 景色 (r-653a43522182); 態 (r-8e7e80c677fe) with 態度 (r-205051be6f37); 怒 (r-88468ea430b0) with 怒る (r-44180d9e2486).
- The 々 card (r-2af09592503a) explains a mark I had been reading in 時々 since Lesson 3, and the four 々 words in this chapter put it to use.
- Pairs that build on known verbs read cleanly: 起こす (r-603aad33a652) against 起きる, 連れる (r-29bd54e8b6f1) against 連れてくる, 早起きする (r-dfd996fa9ce2) from 早 and 起.
- Single kanji follow the deck's usual split: those with one reading (景 kei, 態 tai, 娘 musume) play it, and those with several (向, 織, 空) list them silently.

## Weak spots

**other**

- r-ca49e01ddf43 (差): the readings line says "sa / sa", which looks like a typo until you realise the kun stem matches the on reading (polish).

**unanswerable**

- r-f0b0be3c7a04 (一度): I know 度 as "Degrees (temperature)" and this chapter teaches 三十度 "30 degrees", so "one degree" is a fair reading, but the back only gives "Once" (polish).
