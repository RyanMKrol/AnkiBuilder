# Chapter 05: Lesson 3: Making a Date (chapter-4), learner pass

This is the first chapter where I meet kanji at all: until now I had only the kana unit. It is a lot
at once (48 single kanji and 35 words), but it is built well. Every word breaks down into kanji that
have their own card in this same unit, and the kanji meanings point at the word's meaning: 週 "Week"
plus 末 "End; tip" gives 週末 "Weekend", 映 "To reflect" plus 画 "Picture" gives 映画 "Movie", 毎
"Every" plus 晩 "Nightfall; night" gives 毎晩 "Every night". The verb kanji (食, 飲, 読, 聞, 話, 見,
来, 行, 帰, 起, 寝) each give the kun stem that the verb card then confirms. Nothing collides with a
card I already knew: the kana words from the earlier unit (いま, こんばんは, しんぶん) sit on different
fronts, and they make some of these words easier to remember. The problems I found are all small and
all the same shape: a word whose reading or meaning cannot be built from its parts, where the back
gives the answer but does not tell me the parts were never going to get me there.

## Strong spots

- Kanji and verb pairs line up: 食 "ta, ku" then 食べる taberu (r-d46696735b6a / r-21dac0842106), 飲
  "no" then 飲む nomu (r-5cd375af492e / r-a1ac40e3c709), 聞 "ki" then 聞く kiku (r-d4ff44b74b72 /
  r-abb639bc774f), 帰 "kae" then 帰る kaeru (r-8001d4cacb7b / r-3a9f9a5edc18).
- The meal words share one pattern and teach it three times: 朝ご飯, 昼ご飯, 晩ご飯 with 飯 "Meal;
  boiled rice" (r-04fd047918fb, r-49f334cb28a3, r-a0dede70ceb2, r-00619b09692c).
- 呂 is glossed honestly as "Spine; backbone (mostly used for its sound)" (r-9bcae380442f), so 風
  "Wind" inside お風呂 "Bath" (r-b75b20ed5dc4) does not leave me looking for a meaning that is not
  there.
- 今晩 "Tonight" (r-1666a3e3247d) ties back to こんばんは from the kana unit, and 聞 "bun" ties back
  to しんぶん.
- The days of the week read cleanly: 土曜日 doyōbi and 日曜日 nichiyōbi use readings the 土 and 日
  cards gave me (r-eae85310dc8e, r-ed448a8b8e4d).

## Weak spots

**missing-info (polish)**

- r-574cd4fb795b 今日: the 今 card gives "kon, kin / ima" and the 日 card "ni, nichi, nit-, jitsu /
  bi, hi, ka". No combination makes "kyō", and nothing on the back says this is a whole-word
  reading, so I am left wondering whether I misremembered the kanji cards.
- r-762ffcba27ad 明日: the same problem, worse, because the kanji cards do build a real reading:
  明 "myō" plus 日 "nichi" gives "myōnichi". The back says only "ashita", so reading it from the kanji
  feels like a wrong answer.
- r-44bf896f470f 時々: the 々 mark is not kana and no card has shown it to me. The back's "tokidoki"
  lets me guess that it repeats 時 (and that the repeat changes toki to doki), but nothing confirms it,
  and I will see the mark again.
- r-4a2f5c2aed74 全然: 全 "Whole; entire" plus 然 "So; thus" reads as "entirely", and the back says
  "Not at all". Nothing on the front is negative, so I cannot tell where the "not" comes from or how
  the word is used.
