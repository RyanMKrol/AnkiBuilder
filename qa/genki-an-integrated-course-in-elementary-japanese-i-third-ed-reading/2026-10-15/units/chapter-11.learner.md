# Chapter 12: Lesson 10: Winter Vacation Plans (chapter-11), Learner

This is the biggest kanji load so far: 55 single-kanji cards, nearly all of them abstract ones I have
no way to guess (系, 符, 容, 往, 換, 幹), then 57 words, most of them railway vocabulary. It still
studied well. Every front is the written form alone, every back gives a meaning and a reading, and
the words lean hard on kanji I already had or had just met: 乗 from Lesson 5 for 乗車券 and 乗り換え,
急 from Lesson 6 for 急行 and 特急, 期 for 定期券, 指 for 指定席, 終 and 電 for 終電, 動物 and 園 for
動物園. Railway words that would look alike in English stay apart because the fronts differ. I found
one gloss that drops a distinction the rest of the deck keeps, and one romaji style slip across five
suffix cards. Nothing stops a card from being studied.

## Strong spots

- The temperature set keeps weather and things apart on the back: 冷 "Cold (to the touch)" and
  冷たい "Cold (things; people)" (r-e2fc60d070bd, r-e546e7d633e6), 暖 "Warm (weather)"
  (r-d788044e1425), 涼しい "Cool (weather)" (r-19b9994d115e). Against the earlier 寒い, 暑い, 熱い and
  温 this is exactly the contrast a reader needs.
- The two hayaku words are told apart by their English: 早く "Early; soon" (r-5183903f3825) against
  速く "Fast; quickly" (r-e389584bbdad), matching the earlier 早い / 速い pair.
- The kanji that make up this chapter's words are carded first, so every compound is readable from
  parts I know: 階 + 段 for 階段, 往 + 復 for 往復, 季 + 節 for 季節, 改 + 札 for 改札, 普 + 通 for
  普通, 簡 + 単 for 簡単.
- Readings I could not work out are taught on the back where I need them: 先発 senpatsu
  (r-efa430d2ee11), 切符売り場 kippu uriba (r-14b95d6b2df0), 一号車 ichi-gōsha (r-c344910a8f57),
  学割 gakuwari (r-e31c661ec472).
- The paired platform words 先発 "Departing first" and 次発 "Departing second" (r-efa430d2ee11,
  r-eec9a060f61c) make sense as a pair even met apart.
- 交通系ICカード (r-e07e75baa71b) explains itself with real card names instead of a bare
  translation.

## Weak spots

**missing-info (1, polish)**

- 暖かい (r-c141850f9e8a) says only "Warm", while its kanji 暖 says "Warm (weather)" and the
  earlier 温 says "Warm (to the touch)". It is the one temperature word in the deck that does not
  say which kind it is.

**other (1, polish, unit-level)**

- 方面, 番線, 週間, 行き and か月 (r-c64f974fa90b, r-856d5830b52b, r-ae5d2000edcb, r-cd91af5d05a4,
  r-370f0655dc10) print a leading 〜 in the romaji ("〜hōmen", "〜kagetsu") that the front does
  not show. No earlier reading card does this; the English already marks the slot with "...".

Not reported, because preflight already lists it: the romaji style note on 通 (r-bef21932fe01) and
the acknowledged Latin letters on 交通系ICカード.
