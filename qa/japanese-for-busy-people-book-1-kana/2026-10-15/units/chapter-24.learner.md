# Lesson 24: Work and Interests (learner pass)

This chapter was easy to study. Most of it is two new ています verbs (つとめています "work for" and
すんでいます "live") and the new pair しっています / しりません, practised in short sentences built
from names, places and companies I already knew. The vocabulary is plain loanwords and compounds I
could take apart myself (アパレルメーカー, りょこうがいしゃ, ドイツご), and the notes usually tell me
which earlier word a new one is built from. Most cards worked first time. The one thing that kept
tripping me was よね: every よね card I could answer with the ね I learned in Lesson 6, and nothing on
the front told me otherwise.

## Strong spots

- **The three kinds of "know" are kept apart well.** shitteimasu says しっています is for knowing
  that a person or thing exists, unlike わかります. shirimasen-2 has a hint ("not knowing of a person
  or thing, not failing to understand"). ex8b-dont-know-phone explains why the answer to "do you
  know his phone number?" is わかりません and not しりません. After those three I understood a
  distinction I had never been taught.
- **address-vs-phone-known** ties the は-for-contrast in じゅうしょはしりませんが、でんわばんごうはしっています
  back to the Lesson 23 card スノーボードはしますが、スキーはしません. That is a note I could check
  against what I already knew.
- **つとめます vs つとめています**: the hints on tsutomemasu and tsutometeimasu separate them on
  the front, and the note on tsutomemasu-sentence ("Starting next month, I will work for ABC Foods")
  says when you would use the ます form.
- **tsutometeimasen-sentence**: the note on もう with a negative ("no longer") keeps it apart from
  もういちど and もうすこし.
- **ex8a-ask-andos-work**: the note on おしごとは? says why the sentence can stop at は.
- **The language cards** (doitsugo, chuugokugo, kankokugo) and **nationality-vs-language-q/a**
  make the じん/ご split concrete.
- **Context lines on the short answers** (ex3-yamada-a, ex5-white-a, ex11-suzuki-a,
  no-i-dont-know-chan and others) mean that "I live in X", "It's drawing pictures" and the rest can
  always be answered.

## Weak spots

**ambiguous / unanswerable (should-fix)**

- yone: the Production front "...right? (confirming expression)" is also the meaning of
  chapter-6/ne-particle ("Right?, isn't it?"), and neither card has a hint.
- yone-01, yone-02, yone-03, yone-with-shiru: every English sentence ends in ", right?", which the
  learner was taught to say with ね. Nothing asks for よね.

**leaks-answer (should-fix)**

- musumesan: the Production hint "the plain word, built from むすめ + さん" spells out the answer.

**other (should-fix)**

- sundeimasu: the note says where you live is not said with すみます. But the earlier card
  chapter-17-extras/sumimasu-1 teaches "I live in Tokyo" as わたしはとうきょうにすみます, so the deck
  now marks its own earlier answer as wrong. Preflight already reports the matching English, but not
  this contradiction.

**ambiguous (polish)**

- shirimasen-2: this card has a hint, but chapter-2/wakarimasen ("I don't know") has none, so that
  older card's Production front now has two answers.

**wrong-content (polish)**

- tsutometeimasu: the note says しごとをします is from Lesson 3. It was first taught in Lesson 8.

**missing-info (polish)**

- yoku-shitte-imasu: よく means "well" here, but I only ever learned it as "often", and nothing
  says so.
- ex8b2-ando-mobile-q: the card wants けいたいのばんごう, but I learned けいたいばんごう in Lesson 2.
  Nothing says the two are the same.

**other (polish)**

- ganbatte-kudasai, shitteimasu: the notes say "below", which means nothing once the cards are
  shuffled.
- nationality-vs-language-a, nationality-vs-language-q: the notes name internal card ids.
- ex10-1-q-kato-english, ex10-2-q-sasaki-chinese: the notes describe "the chapter's picture",
  which I never see.
