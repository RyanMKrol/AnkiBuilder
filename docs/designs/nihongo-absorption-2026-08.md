# Absorbing Nihongo 101 and Nihongo 102 into the book collection

**Status:** approved 2026-08-24. Routing table signed off; execution in progress.

## Why

Two Japanese collections were being studied daily and overlapped heavily:

- `output/epubs/japanese-for-busy-people-book-1-kana/`, 17 base units and 16 `-extras`
  siblings, 2,159 delivered cards.
- `output/courses/nihongo-101-course-n5/`, three lessons, 236 delivered cards.

102 of the course's 236 cards (43%) were already in the book with identical Japanese, so they
were being studied twice. The course was also stalled: three lessons deep, `lesson-0` carrying a
`courseSlug` from an abandoned course, nothing added since.

This migration ends with one product. The course's genuinely new material moves into the chapter
it belongs to, the redundant half is retired, and the Nihongo 101 deck goes away. Two PDFs of
Nihongo 102 class notes are folded into the same place.

## The isolation rule

CLAUDE.md golden rule 7 forbids comparing or deduping two collections against each other. This
migration does exactly that, once, and it is sanctioned because it **dissolves** the boundary
rather than crossing it: afterwards there is one collection and the rule holds again with nothing
left to compare. It is not a precedent. See the LIMITATIONS entry.

## Method

Routing is by judgement, not by a heuristic. The English gloss list of all 17 base units was read
to establish what each chapter genuinely teaches, and each of the 290 items was placed against
that. One mechanical rule constrains every placement, from
`.claude/skills/build-anki-deck/references/extras-pass.md`: a card may use only vocabulary and
grammar from its chapter or an earlier one. Where topical fit and prerequisite disagreed,
prerequisite won. That is why `しゅっしんはどこですか` sits at Lesson 5 rather than with the other
introductions: Lesson 1 teaches only the polite `どちら`.

Duplicate detection filters `excluded` on both sides. Not doing so overcounts, which is how an
early pass reported 104 duplicates instead of 102.

## Resolved before execution

Eleven items were flagged for a decision. All were resolved on 2026-08-24.

- **`scarf`**: originally flagged as a card-id collision needing a re-id. On checking the book it
  turned out to be simpler than that: the book already ships マフラー as `mafuraa` in
  chapter-14, so the course card is a plain duplicate and is dropped. No re-id, no re-tag.
- **`irl-l1-39`**: the target carried a literal `[number]`, which preflight's `placeholder-target`
  check fails. Rewritten to a concrete number in the book's own style, kana digits joined by `の`
  for each hyphen.
- **`どうやって〜にいきますか`**: a shipped `〜` is a preflight failure. Rewritten around `ジム`.
- **`なんですか`**: appears in both the course and the PDF. The course card `irl-l1-38` ships
  because it has review history and audio; the PDF copy is dropped.
- **`かんこく`, `きのう`**: the book has both, but its own reviewer excluded them, so they are not
  duplicates. They move as new content.
- **`ear`, `eye`, `fire`, `star`, `celebrity`**: no chapter fits. Parked with Lesson 12, and
  Lesson 15 for `celebrity`.

## Found during authoring

Two things the exact-target duplicate match could not see, both caught before anything was merged.

- **`じゃあまた` is a duplicate after all.** The book teaches the same expression in chapter-0 as
  `ja-mata` (`じゃ、また`, "See you."), spelled with a comma and a short vowel. A sweep that also
  normalizes away `、。？！` and a leading honorific `お`/`ご` found it. The course card is dropped
  like any other duplicate. The same sweep raised `ごふん` against the counter `ふん`, which is a
  false positive: the `ご` there is the number five, not an honorific.
- **`のみもの` needs a cue, and so does the book card it collides with.** The book has `おのみもの`
  ("Beverage", chapter-9), the polite form a restaurant uses. Both stay, and both need a hint. The
  same applies to `かっこいい` against the book's `すずしい`, which are both glossed "Cool" and mean
  different things.

One card was authored that appears in neither source: `かいしゃにいきました`. The PDF prints the
`いきます` paradigm as a table, and `extras-pass.md` requires a paradigm to ship as a full fixed-frame
contrast set, so shipping three of its four cells would be the "carded half-way" failure that file
names.

## Card-id collisions: all eleven fall on dropped cards

A card id becomes the `abid` tag the delivery tool matches on, so a course card moving under an id
the book already uses would silently bind to the wrong note. Course and book share eleven ids:
`baseball`, `beef`, `beer`, `black`, `camera`, `file`, `pen`, `restroom`, `scarf`, `small`,
`white`. **Every one of them is on a card being dropped as a duplicate**, so none of the 134 moved
cards carries a colliding id and no re-id is needed anywhere in this migration.

Re-derive with the check in the Verification section before Phase 5. If a future change turns any
of those eleven back into a move, this conclusion expires.

## Deviation from the extras authoring rules

`extras-pass.md` says "Do not add bare vocabulary cards, numbers, or counter recitations." Many of
the incoming course cards are exactly that. That rule governs authoring new padding; these are
pre-existing cards with review history being relocated, and dropping them would delete studied
content. They move as they are, each stamped with a `reviewNote` recording its origin. Recorded in
LIMITATIONS.

## How the merge ran

Phase 3 is `scripts/absorb-nihongo.mjs`, a spent one-off migration kept for the record. It reads
this document's routing table and cards file, appends the routed cards to each destination unit's
`cards.json`, mirrors them into `corpus.json` in the same order, copies each moved card's audio takes
across (including hand-trimmed manual ones), and adds the two hint pairs the collision analysis
requires. It is idempotent: a card already present by id is skipped.

Moved cards keep their original card id. That is load-bearing rather than incidental: the id becomes
the `abid:` tag `deliver-to-anki.mjs` matches notes by, so keeping it is what lets the live Anki note
be found and updated in place instead of created fresh with no review history.

Before it ran, all 15 destination units had `meta.done` cleared with `scripts/undone-unit.mjs` and
their corpus sign-off withdrawn, so the added cards face both review gates rather than shipping
unreviewed. Base units were deliberately NOT un-reviewed: a base unit's sign-off owns its
dedup-library entry, and withdrawing it would delete the entry every later chapter's backward dedup
reads. The two book-side hints are therefore a field-only edit.

## Moving the live Anki notes

Phase 5 is `scripts/migrate-nihongo-absorption.mjs`, also a spent one-off. `deliver-to-anki.mjs`
matches a note to a card by its durable `abid:<card.id>` tag, but it looks with one query scoped to
the collection's own parent deck. A note under `Nihongo 101 Course (N5)` is therefore invisible to the
book's delivery: the card would read as new, be added with fresh scheduling, and the matured original
would only be reported as orphaned. Moving the notes into the book's tree first is what makes the
next deliver find them and update them in place.

The safety design is copied from `scripts/migrate-deck-numbering.mjs`, which did the same kind of
move on this collection in August. Every affected card's `due`, `ivl`, `factor`, `reps`, `lapses`,
`queue` and `type` are snapshotted, re-read after the move, and compared field by field; any drift
aborts the run. A card in a filtered deck is skipped and reported rather than moved, because what
`changeDeck` does to one is the unanswered `change-deck-on-filtered` probe. `--dry` is the default.

The dry run on 2026-08-24 resolved all 133 moved cards by `abid`, with none unresolved and none
filtered: 266 cards across 15 destination decks.
