# Lesson 17 (chapter-17 + chapter-17-extras): learner pass

This chapter was easy to study and mostly clean. たい is taught as a full grid for five verbs
(want, don't want, wanted, didn't want), and the notes tie each form back to the い-adjective
endings from Lesson 11, so the pattern made sense straight away. まえに is taught with nouns and
with verbs, and the extras unit gives plenty of short sentences that use both. Nearly every
sentence card could be answered from earlier lessons plus this chapter's words. The problems are
few: one hint that hands over the answer, a set of seven notes that overstate the まえに rule, two
authored sentences I could not trust (すみます for "I live", ごじのまえに), and one あの card glossed
without "over there".

## Strong spots

- The たい grid in the base unit (`kaitai-desu-2` through `aitakunakatta-desu`) is consistent, and
  `ikitakunai-desu`, `ikitakatta-desu` and `ikitakunakatta-desu` explain each ending by the
  adjective pattern I already know.
- `aitai-desu` reminds me that あいます keeps its に with たい, and the extras use it every time
  (`aitai-1`, `aitakatta-3`, `aitakunai-3`).
- `nomitai-desu` tells me the object can take が. `kaitakunai-3` explains why なにも works with
  たくない. Both answered a question I would have had.
- `biyouin-2` warns about びょういん, `sumimasu-2` warns about すみません, `kakimasu` separates write
  from draw, and `mae` separates "before" from "in front". Each is a confusion I would have made.
- `ryouri-o-shimasu` ("the activity, not the person") and `oyogimasu` ("not the noun-plus-suru
  form") have hints that rule out コック and すいえいをします.
- `want-to-come-japan` explains きたい against くたい, and `cherry-blossom-good-place-ask` explains
  したい. The irregular stems are covered.
- `future-where-want-to-live-a` has a scene that tells me it is an answer, so the front is fair.

## Weak spots

**leaks-answer (should-fix)**

- `ikitai-desu`: the Production hint "Drop ます from いきます, add たいです" spells the answer.

**wrong-content (should-fix)**

- Extras unit: seven notes (`ikebana-wo-narau-mae-ni-hon`, `kuruma-wo-arau-mae-ni-hirugohan`,
  `shawaa-wo-abiru-mae-ni-kohii`, `kaigi-wo-hajimeru-mae-ni-kohii`,
  `toukyou-ni-sumu-mae-ni-uchi-wo-mitai`, `daunroodo-suru-mae-ni-mitai`,
  `meeru-wo-chekku-suru-mae-ni-asagohan`) say まえに "always follows the dictionary form". The
  deck's own かいぎのまえに and パーティーのまえに cards say otherwise.
- `sumimasu-1`: "I live in Tokyo." for とうきょうにすみます. Every ます verb I know is a habit or a
  plan, so this reads to me as "I will live in Tokyo".

**note-unverifiable (should-fix)**

- `mae-time-go-home`: the note says any time word takes の before まえに (ごじのまえに). The card
  is authored and I have nothing to check the rule against.

**unanswerable (should-fix)**

- `eat-at-restaurant-answer`: "that restaurant" points to その. The deck always says "over there"
  for あの.

**ambiguous (polish)**

- Base unit: `dansu`, `zehi-l17`, `wakarimashita-l17`, `oyogimasu` and `shokuji-o-shimasu` repeat
  words I already have cards for, with the same answer.
- `neru-mae-ni-stretch` and `smith-stretch-before-sleep` differ only by まいにち.

**missing-info / untaught-prerequisite (polish)**

- `kaitai-x2`: おみやげに ("as a souvenir") is a use of に I was never taught.
- `zehi-3`: みてください uses みて, a form no card taught.

**other (polish)**

- `mae-before-verb-station` and `mae-front-station`: notes point at "the next item" and "the
  previous item".

I did not report what preflight already lists: the dictionary-form derivation notes, the romaji
in the まえに notes, the long Production faces, the two "so I don't want to" cards that read as
replies, the `kakimasu` same-word note and the `neru-mae-ni-terebi-mitai` composition note.
