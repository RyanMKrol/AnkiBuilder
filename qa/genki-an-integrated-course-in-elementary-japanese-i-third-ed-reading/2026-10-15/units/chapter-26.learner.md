# Chapter 00: Kana (chapter-26), Learner

This is the first unit, so I came to it knowing nothing but kana. It was easy to study. Every front
is one kana word I can sound out, and every back gives a plain English meaning I can learn from the
card alone. Nothing needs a word I have not met, because there is nothing before it. There are no
notes on any card, and almost none are needed: the words are everyday nouns, loanwords, numbers,
times and set greetings, and the English is clear. I found three small things in 299 cards.

## Strong spots

- **Near pairs are told apart on the back.** しゅじん "Husband" (r-cef4fee4bd6d) and しゅうじん
  "Prisoner" (r-180971530c6c) drill the long vowel. ハンバーガー "Hamburger" (r-cba9cd9ee9e3) and
  ハンバーグ "Hamburger steak" (r-d146b38f5839) are clearly different dishes. りょう "Dormitory"
  (r-1e91a7525d73) and りょうり "Cooking; dish" (r-e437be4fbe8f) don't get confused.
- **Polite and plain greetings are marked.** おはよう / おはようございます "(polite)"
  (r-2d6e22246673, r-5a3c5f71eeac), ありがとう / ありがとうございます (r-42d0969029e9,
  r-24afcca10e22), いただきます "(before eating)" and ごちそうさまでした "(after eating)"
  (r-25567f8581d3, r-11c8fca7e223), すみません and しつれいします with its use spelled out
  (r-cce1862bcbd9, r-dfc3bb392e50).
- **Both readings of the numbers are carded.** よん / し (r-ceb4a54e2de4, r-812f45bfcf7e), なな / しち,
  きゅう / く, れい / ゼロ, and the teens built from each. They share an English answer, but on a
  silent reading card the front decides, so they never collide. The sound changes come with them:
  いっぷん, じゅうろっぷん, ろっぴゃく, はっぴゃく, さんびゃく, さんぜん.
- **Glosses that could mislead are pinned down.** デート "Date (romantic, not calendar)"
  (r-71911cd8dbd2), ホーム "Platform (at a train station)" (r-ed28cf13bde2), ひく "To catch (a cold)"
  (r-a2d3fe43375c), クラシック "Classical music" (r-ea12219d0ec1).

## Weak spots

**missing-info**

- LLサイズ (r-f23f878f91a8) and XLサイズ (r-09614a66bafb) both answer "Size XL". Nothing tells me
  LL is the Japanese label for extra large, so I assume one of the two is a mistake. (polish)
- きれい (r-2b8c515b4475) is carded without な, while げんきな, ひまな, しずかな, たいへんな and
  にぎやかな all carry it. Because it ends in い, I put it with いい and かゆい. (polish)

**wrong-content**

- ゴールデンウィーク (r-36cbc6b98422) says "gōruden'wīku". The apostrophe after n before w doesn't
  appear anywhere else in the deck (こんばんは is "konbanwa"). (polish)

Preflight already flags the Latin-letter fronts, the single-kana fronts and the phrase-sized fronts
and has acknowledged them, so I have not reported them again.
