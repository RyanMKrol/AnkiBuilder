# Lesson 16 (chapter-16 + chapter-16-extras): learner pass

Studying this chapter felt good. Every invitation verb comes in a ませんか / ましょう pair, the
four station exits are built the same way, and the extras unit puts all of it into short, realistic
exchanges: invite, accept or decline, pick a time, pick a place. Nearly every sentence card could be
answered from what earlier lessons taught plus this chapter's vocabulary, and the hints on the
declining and accepting cards did their job. The trouble was all on single-word Production fronts,
where the English is also the gloss of a word I already know (Next, Festival/event, A bit,
How about you?, Let's do it/that). There are also a handful of notes that point "above" or "below",
or cite the book's GRAMMAR numbers.

## Strong spots

- The accept/decline set has scenes that separate cards that would otherwise collide:
  `ee-zehi`, `iidesune-zehi`, `sp1-arigatou-zehi`, `chotto-decline`, `decline-anime`,
  `x16a-zannen-kinyoubi-chotto`.
- The two senses of あります are kept apart well: `arimasu-takeplace` (で, takes place) against
  `x16a-sobaya-kara-ikimasenka` (に, exists), and `x16a-pooru-suzuki-event-ikimasu` explains why
  the event takes に when you go to it.
- `onaka-suki` warns that すきました is not the すき of Lesson 15. That is exactly the confusion I
  would have had.
- `x16r-kimasenka` and `sp1-party-invite` make the きませんか / いきませんか choice clear.
- The meeting-place sentences (`ex7-shichiji-kara`, `x16c-rokuji-han-higashiguchi`,
  `x16c-juuji-minamiguchi`, `x16c-kitaguchi-aimasenka`) each use a different exit, so each one
  reinforces the exit words.
- `x16a-smith-mo-dou-desu-ka` and `x16r-ja-nichiyoubi-dou-desu-ka` show what どうですか is
  actually for, better than its own vocabulary card does.

## Weak spots

**ambiguous / unanswerable (should-fix)**

- `tsugi-next`: "Next" is also こんど's gloss (Lesson 9).
- `taikai`: "Festival, event" fits おまつり (Lesson 7) and イベント (this chapter).
- `sukoshi`: "A bit, a little" is ちょっと (Lesson 10). The note admits they mean the same thing.
- `sou-shimashou` and `shimashou`: "Let's do that." and "Let's do it." are too close to tell apart.
- `dou-desu-ka`: "How about you?" is how I learned Xさんは?. The chapter uses どうですか for
  "how about it / how about Sunday".

**unanswerable (polish)**

- `x16a-tsugi-akihabara-event` and `x16a-pooru-suzuki-event-ikimasu`: "next Sunday" could just as
  well be らいしゅうの or こんどのにちようび.

**wrong-content / other (polish)**

- `mimashou` / `mimasenka`: "Let's see." and "Shall we see?" read as English fillers meaning
  "let me think", not "watch".
- `shimasenka`: "Shall we do?" is not a whole English sentence.
- `kara-because`: the note says because-から is a new job. Lesson 14 already taught it.
- `yukimatsuri`: the note cites "Key Sentence 1". I learned おまつり in Lesson 7.
- Base unit: positional and drill-grid notes on `ikimasenka`, `ikimashou-vocab` and `mimashou`.
- Extras: positional notes on `x16a-event-nanji-kara` and `x16c-kitaguchi-aimasenka`. Book GRAMMAR n
  citations on `x16a-tsugi-akihabara-event` and `ks1-omatsuri` through `ks4-tenki-kara`.

I did not report anything preflight already lists: the ぐち and まつり compositions, the
すきます derivation, `zannen-desu` answerable alone, the long Production faces and the three
"doesn't really work for me" refusals.
