**Grammar:**

- G1: place で event が あります ("take place"; the place takes で)
- G2: verb stem + ませんか to invite
- G2a: accepting: ええ / はい、ぜひ; いいですね。ぜひ; ええ / はい、そう しましょう
- G2b: declining: すみません。N は ちょっと…; ざんねんですが、N は ちょっと…, and why the
  sentence is left unfinished
- G3: verb stem + ましょう, "let's"
- G4: clause1 から、clause2 (reason first)

**Notes:**

- N1: どうですか asks the listener's intention
- N2: 15にちの にちようび: "date の day of the week", date first, joined by の (apposition)
- N3: きませんか for inviting someone to your home or to your own event
- N4: ありがとうございます。ぜひ when accepting an invitation to the speaker's home or planned event

**Key Sentences:** KS1 to KS4

## Findings

### book-gap

- **chapter-16-extras/-** (should-fix): Speaking Practice NOTES teach that a date and a day of the
  week are joined as "date の day of the week", date first (15にちの にちようび). It appears only
  inside sp1-party-invite, whose note is about きませんか. No card explains it.

### book-mismatch

- **chapter-16/mata-kondo** (should-fix): the hint ("a soft way to turn down an invitation") and
  the note ("used to gently defer an invitation") cast また こんど as the invitee's refusal. In the
  book the inviter says it after being turned down: "B: ざんねんですが、にちようびは ちょっと…。
  A: そうですか。じゃ、また こんど。" extras/x16a-sou-desu-ka-mata-kondo gets it right.
- **chapter-16-extras/x16a-tsugi-akihabara-event** (should-fix): the note says "Time takes に as
  always". Lesson 6 GRAMMAR 3 says relative time expressions (あした, らいしゅう, こんげつ...)
  "generally do not take particles", and this deck ships らいげつさっぽろで…, らいしゅうサッカー…
  and こんばんしょくじを… with no に.
- **chapter-16-extras/x16c-onaka-ne-tabemasenka**, **fib-nodo-nanika-nomimasenka**,
  **fib-tsukare-sukoshi-yasumimasenka** (should-fix, one finding each): おなかがすきましたね /
  のどがかわきましたね / つかれましたね are rendered "You're hungry / thirsty / tired, aren't
  you?". The book glosses the phrases "I'm hungry / I'm thirsty / I'm tired", and in Exercise VI
  the speaker gives their own state as the reason to invite. The English moves the feeling onto
  the listener.
- **chapter-16/kara-because** (polish): the note calls this から "a different job from the から you
  already know for 'from'", as if "because" were new. Lesson 14 NOTES 2 already taught から
  "because" (かいぎが ありますから), and the deck carded it as chapter-14/kara-particle-because.
- **chapter-16-extras/x16a-pooru-suzuki-event-ikimasu** (polish): the book's closing line is
  "すずきさんと ポールさんは…" / "Suzuki-san and Paul-san…"; the card swaps the order to
  ポールさんとすずきさんは / "Paul-san and Suzuki-san" while citing the book as its source.

### note-unverifiable

- **chapter-16-extras/decline-doyoubi** (polish): "ざんねんですが softens the refusal more than
  すみません". The book makes no ranking; it pairs すみません with "not really [to my taste]" and
  ざんねんですが with "a little [inconvenient for me]". The note's second half (scheduling conflict
  vs taste) is supported.

### other

- **chapter-16/mimashou** (polish): "Let's see." reads as the English idiom for "let me think". The
  table column means "see / watch", so "Let's watch (it)." would avoid the wrong prompt on the
  Production face.
- **chapter-16/-** (polish, metadata only): mimashou, fib-nodo-sou-shimashou and x16r-kimasenka
  carry `aiSuggested`, but the book prints all three (WORD POWER IV, GRAMMAR 2 "ええ / はい、そう
  しましょう", Speaking Practice NOTES).
