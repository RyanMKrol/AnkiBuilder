# Chapter 21: Reading and Writing 7: Mary's Letter (chapter-20), Learner

A short, comfortable chapter. Most of the 30 cards are compounds built from kanji I already had:
社会, 高校生, 小学生, 文化, 文学, 英語, 会議 and the rest could be read off parts the ledger taught
(社, 会, 高, 校, 生, 小, 学, 文, 化). The five new single kanji (最, 宅, 議, 英, 京) each sit next to a
compound that uses them (最高, 帰宅, 会議, 英語, 上京する), so every new character is met twice in the
same chapter and the compound immediately gives it a job. The fronts are all single words and none
gives anything away. Only two cards left me wanting something, both small.

## Strong spots

- New kanji arrive with a compound to anchor them: 最 with 最高 (r-dfa685b3855d, r-2cd489b0a572),
  宅 with 帰宅 (r-8e3089407539, r-55ee3cafb21f), 議 with 会議 (r-1361c93411ea, r-1fcc3dd3c573),
  英 with 英語 (r-c7acd3b00e99, r-9e84046cc13f), 京 with 上京する (r-812aba4f006d, r-547f0abd83b7).
- Pairs with earlier cards make good contrast without colliding: 女子学生 against the earlier 男子学生
  (r-ea5b3a8abe26), 輸入 against 輸出 (r-b8f7009a7bb8), 帰国 / 帰宅 / 帰り against 帰る
  (r-56083666cb77, r-55ee3cafb21f, r-8066f371e59e), 母語 next to 父母 (r-9bbc85b6e0f5, r-4590bfff3e78).
- 気をつける (r-ecda751ef69a) stands well as a word card after the phrase card was cut; 気 was
  already taught, so the front is readable.
- Single-kanji readings follow the same on / kun stem layout as earlier chapters (最 "sai / motto",
  like 帰 "ki / kae").

## Weak spots

**missing-info**

- r-4d564a3bda7f 毎年: the back shows only "mainen", although the book gives まいとし too. A learner
  who reads it maitoshi (natural after 今年 kotoshi) is told nothing that confirms it. Polish.

**other (gloss)**

- r-b78237178873 入社: "Entry to a company" sounds like a physical entrance, which is what 入口 already
  means, so the wrong guess "company entrance" feels confirmed. "Joining a company" would fix it.
  Polish.
