# Chapter 08: Lesson 6: A Day in Robert's Life (chapter-7), learner pass

This was a heavy chapter but a fair one. It has 53 new single kanji and 44 words, and every word is
built only from kanji I either met in an earlier unit or meet as a kanji card in this one. I checked
each word against the ledger and this unit's kanji cards: 幼稚園, 保育園, 短期大学, 高等学校, 信号,
左側, 結構 and the rest all break down into kanji I have a card for. Where a word uses a reading the
kanji card did not give me (中 was taught as "naka" only in chapter-5, and 中学校 needs "chū"), the
word's back gives the romaji, so I learn the new reading at that point, which is how it should work.
The kanji meanings match how the words use them (変 "Strange; change" inside 大変, 当 "Hit; correct"
inside 本当, 等 "Grade; etc." inside 高等学校). No front repeats one I already know, so nothing
collides. I found one small problem.

## Strong spots

- The kanji cards and the word cards support each other. 忘 then 忘れる, 返 then 返す, 借 then
  借りる, 浴 then 浴びる: the kanji card gives the meaning and the kun stem (wasu, kae, ka, a), and
  the word card confirms it (r-178747962b40 / r-4da7571d829d, r-9a0fe9c816a8 / r-a76072644bf6,
  r-83f88c8f0e30 / r-5ba9a2f65988, r-8ae4844630a6 / r-fb2046cced43).
- The pairs a learner tends to mix up are told apart on the back: 連れてくる "To bring (a person)"
  against 持ってくる "To bring (a thing)" (r-b8b45b0fe728, r-2f01ad346d69), and 開ける / 閉める both
  marked "(something)" (r-8d2086f34297, r-16ae24721e14).
- The school ladder reads cleanly from kanji I have: 幼稚園, 保育園, 小学校, 中学校, 高等学校,
  短期大学, 大学院 (r-132fa45a7a57, r-ff9f892a080a, r-327cdf816423, r-2e5fecd2cc7f, r-4d2e8f09ab66,
  r-365a0d35af38, r-6293f3d771e4).
- 浴びる "To take (a shower); to bathe in" (r-fb2046cced43) ties straight back to シャワー from the
  kana unit.

## Weak spots

**missing-info (polish)**

- r-c1f5acd9d1eb 曲がる: the kanji card r-0a82dc79f717 teaches 曲 as "Bend", so reading the word I
  answer "to bend"; the back says only "To turn". Both are right, but the back does not confirm the
  meaning the kanji led me to, and "turn" alone does not say it is turning a corner or a direction.
  A wider gloss would fix it.
