## Findings

  the model sentence for GRAMMAR 2; the card ships the contracted タクシーのりばはえきのまえです.
- chapter-13-extras/fib-shachoushitsu-kaigishitsu-tonari (should-fix): a が existence sentence
  glossed with the は-sentence English ("The meeting room is next to..."), the opposite of how
  GRAMMAR 2 translates ① and ②.
- chapter-13/osobayasan (polish): the hint glosses さん as the "Mr./Ms." ending; NOTES 1 says さん after
  〜や softens the tone.
- chapter-13/fib-fork-table-count-a (polish): writes 10ほん where the book's exercise prints 10 ぽん,
  and where the rest of this deck spells the sound change (１ぽん, ３ぼん, 20ぷん).
