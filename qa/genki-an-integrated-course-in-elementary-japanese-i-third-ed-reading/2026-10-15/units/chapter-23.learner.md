# Chapter 24: Reading and Writing 10: The Folktale Kasajizo (chapter-23), Learner

A short, comfortable chapter of 26 cards. Five new characters (戸, 声, 移, 解, 販) open it, and
almost everything after that is a compound read from characters I already held: 自 and 動 and 車
from earlier lessons give 自動車, 夜 gives three words, 売 and 買 give 売買, and the new 販, 解 and
移 each come back straight away in 自動販売機, 正解 and 移住する. A few compounds use a reading of a
known character that its own card never gave me (夜 as ya and yo, 所 and 雪 as sho and setsu, 町 as
chō), but each back teaches the reading, which is how this deck works. Nothing on any card is wrong
as far as the deck has taught me. I have one polish finding.

## Strong spots

- **New characters come back at once.** 販 (r-2ea8aba2d5fc) is used in 自動販売機
  (r-ce2dfb7e9795); 解 (r-d2feb31d47eb) in 正解 (r-5c99dc42f8af); 移 (r-c59a75dc9cf3) in 移住する
  (r-5ad8d88db860).
- **Building from earlier units.** 自動車 (r-8e67e6a00e7b) reads straight off 自 "Self", 動
  "Move; change" and 車 "Car". 自由 (r-4c974ea89d43) follows 自由席, and 私立高校 (r-bc299feb4e33)
  follows 私立大学 and 高校, so its shiritsu reading was already familiar. 国立大学
  (r-288738699dc6) pairs with it neatly.
- **A near-twin is marked.** 今夜 "Tonight (formal)" (r-6771993f14db) sits apart from the Lesson 3
  card 今晩 "Tonight", so the back never left me unsure which word I had.
- **Kun and on side by side.** 売る uru (r-e2952d33ea3a) and 売買 baibai (r-a79c69dbe6c0) show both
  readings the 売 card gave me (bai / u), and 正しい (r-ffb400348758) next to 正解 does the same for 正.

## Weak spots

**other**

- 移住する (r-5ad8d88db860): the back says "To immigrate". The parts I was taught, 移 "Move" and
  住 "Live (reside)", read as "move and settle somewhere", which covers moving abroad in either
  direction or moving within a country. "Immigrate" picks only moving into a country, so I would
  not recognise the word used for emigrating or for moving to the countryside.

Two things I noticed and did not count as findings. 販 "Sell" and 売 "To sell" are both single
characters meaning sell, and nothing tells me how they differ, but the compounds (自動販売機 and
売る, 売買) show where each one is used. 販 has audio while 移 and 解 are silent; preflight passes
that split and earlier units have done the same.
