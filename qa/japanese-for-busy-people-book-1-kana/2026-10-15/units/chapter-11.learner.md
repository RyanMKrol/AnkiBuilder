# Lesson 11 (chapter-11 + chapter-11-extras): learner pass

This chapter was easy to study. It teaches the past of adjectives (かったです, くなかったです, でした, じゃありませんでした), が meaning "but", ぐらい and どのくらい, and a few visiting phrases. Most cards just put this lesson's endings on adjectives I learned in Lesson 10 (おおきい, おいしい, あつい, きれい, にぎやか), so I could answer almost every sentence from the ledger and the word cards. The extras go through each new adjective (つまらない, いそがしい, たいへん, かんたん) in all four forms, and the scene hints on short replies tell me which question each one answers. The problems are small and they repeat. One front gives its answer away. Two fronts use an English word the deck already linked to a different Japanese word ("How", "Really?"). Three cards translate とても as "really" when the deck taught me ほんとうに for that. And several notes use the word "plain" for forms the deck has always shown me as ordinary です and ます.

## Strong spots

- **The two よかったです cards.** yokatta-desu-good ("describing how something you experienced turned out") and yokatta-desu-glad ("hearing a positive response after giving a present...") share a target and their hints keep them apart in both directions. v11-sou-desu-ka-yokatta-desu adds a note that the "glad" use isn't describing the souvenir.
- **Adjective paradigms.** ookii-desu / ookikunai-desu / ookikatta-desu / ookikunakatta-desu, and the f11 sets for つまらない, いそがしい, たいへん, かんたん, ひま and むずかしい. Every tense and polarity is clear from the English.
- **The つまらない notes.** f11-kono-hon-wa-tsumaranai-desu, f11-kono-hon-wa-tsumaranakunai-desu and f11-kono-hon-wa-tsumaranakatta-desu all warn that the ない belongs to the word itself. That is exactly where I would have tripped.
- **Irregular いい.** ookikatta-desu's note contrasts よかったです with いかったです, and yokunakatta-desu and ex11-amari-yokunakatta-desu drill the form.
- **Reply hints.** fib-hai-muzukashikatta-desu, ex11-hai-yasukatta-desu, ex11-iie-omoshirokunakatta-desu, amari-tanoshikunakatta and ex11-totemo-tanoshikatta-desu each say which question they answer.
- **が contrast sentences.** kinou-isogashii-kyou-hima, ex11-kinou-wa-hima-deshita-ga-kyou-wa-isogashii-desu, ex11-taihen-deshita-ga-tanoshikatta-desu and the two weather pairs show が joining clauses that use words I already know.
- **Useful notes.** misemasu (not みます), taihen-na (versus むずかしい), and ex11-sumisu-san-kinou-wa-omiyage-o-arigatou-gozaimashita (why it's きのうは and not きのうの).

## Weak spots

**ambiguous**

- chapter-11/dou (should-fix): "How (in question)" also fits どうやって, which Lesson 7 taught as "How".
- chapter-11-extras/v11-sou-desu-ka-yokatta-desu (should-fix): the English starts "Really?", which is the deck's gloss for ほんとうですか, but the answer is そうですか.
- chapter-11/fib-totemo-yokatta-desu-concert, chapter-11-extras/ex11-totemo-oishikatta-desu, chapter-11-extras/ex11-totemo-kirei-deshita (polish): each translates とても as "really", which sends me to ほんとうに. In the same unit, ex11-hontou-ni-kirei-desu-ne uses "really" for ほんとうに.

**leaks-answer**

- chapter-11/jikan-suffix (should-fix): the Production hint "as in 2じかん (ni-jikan)" prints the answer.

**missing-info**

- chapter-11-extras/ex11-kore-hakone-no-omiyage-desu (should-fix): with no scene, I would say これは..., and the card's own note says that's the wrong version here.
- chapter-11-extras/ex11-hontou-ni-kirei-desu-ne (polish): the note talks about Sasaki, Emma and a photo, and none of them are on the card.
- chapter-11/deeto (polish): the bare "Date" could mean a calendar date.

**note-unverifiable**

- chapter-11/arukimasu (should-fix): calls あるきます the "Dictionary/plain form", but it has the same ます ending as every verb I've learned.
- chapter-11/kirei-deshita, chapter-11/taihen-na, chapter-11/tanoshii (polish): call でした and かったです a "plain past form". The deck never says what "plain" means, and these are the same polite pasts I learned.

Preflight already flagged the dou-deshita-ka and kirei-ja-arimasen-deshita note claims, the answerable-alone fronts (ookii-desu, kirei-desu and others), the romaji style items and the long Production fronts, so this report doesn't repeat them.
