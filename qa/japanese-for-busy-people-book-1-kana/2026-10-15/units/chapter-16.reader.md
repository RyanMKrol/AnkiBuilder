- N3: きませんか for inviting someone to your home or your own event

## Findings

### book-gap

- **chapter-16-extras/- (sp1-party-invite)** (should-fix): the Speaking Practice NOTE that a date
  and day of the week are joined as "date の day", date first (15にちの にちようび), appears only
  inside a sentence. No card explains it.

### book-mismatch

- **chapter-16/mata-kondo** (should-fix): the hint ("a soft way to turn down an invitation") and the
  note ("gently defer an invitation") cast また こんど as the invitee's refusal. In the book the
  inviter says it, after being turned down: "B: ざんねんですが、にちようびは ちょっと…。 A: そうですか。
  じゃ、また こんど。" (Extras x16a-sou-desu-ka-mata-kondo gets this right.)
- **chapter-16-extras/x16a-tsugi-akihabara-event** (should-fix): the note says "Time takes に as
  always". The book says relative time expressions "generally do not take particles", and this deck
  ships らいげつさっぽろで… with no に.
- **chapter-16/kara-because** (polish): the note calls this から "a different job from the から you
  already know for 'from'". But Lesson 14 NOTES 2 already taught から "because", and the deck carded
  it there (chapter-14/kara-particle-because).

### wrong-content

- **chapter-16-extras/x16c-onaka-ne-tabemasenka**, **fib-nodo-nanika-nomimasenka**,
  **fib-tsukare-sukoshi-yasumimasenka** (should-fix, one finding each): おなかがすきましたね /
  のどがかわきましたね / つかれましたね are rendered "You're hungry/thirsty/tired, aren't you?". The book
  glosses each phrase as "I'm hungry / thirsty / tired". In Exercise VI the speaker gives their own
  state as the reason to invite, so the English moves the feeling onto the listener.
- **chapter-16/mimashou** (polish): "Let's see." is the English idiom for "let me think". The book's
  table column means "see / watch", so "Let's watch (it)." would be clearer.
