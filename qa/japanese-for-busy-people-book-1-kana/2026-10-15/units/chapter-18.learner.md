# Lesson 18: Explaining Plans (learner pass)

This chapter is the て-form, and it is a pleasant one to study. The base unit drills the て-form of every verb I already know, one card each, and the notes group them by ending (い/ち/り give って, み gives んで, き gives いて, ぎ gives いで, し gives して), so by the end of the drill I can derive a form I have not seen yet. The extras then use those forms in short "do X and then Y" sentences built almost entirely from vocabulary I have had for weeks, and the many "Answering: What are you going to do tomorrow?" scenes make the chained sentences easy to place. Most of the problems are in the よろしい cards and in two particle choices the cards never explain.

## Strong spots

- The て-form drill (te-form-kau, te-form-matsu, te-form-kaeru, te-form-kaku, te-form-oyogu, te-form-nomu, te-form-kasu, te-form-yomu, te-form-hanasu, te-form-kiku, te-form-au) gives a sound rule and a sibling verb on every back, so the cards check each other.
- The confusable pairs are named on the back where I would mix them up: いて and いって (te-form-iru, itte-2), みて and みせて (te-form-miru, te-form-miseru), して and かして (te-form-suru).
- answer-osaka-branch-presentation says plainly that the last verb sets the tense, the one thing I needed for the past-tense chains (went-ginza-ate-lunch, past-chain-swim, tomodachi-atte-sumo-yesterday).
- chotto-yoroshii-desu-ka has a clear scene ("to a busy boss or someone senior") that tells me which register to use.
- The link-* cards each pair one new て-form with a short note, which makes them quick reviews of the drill.

## Weak spots

**Ambiguous**

- ato: the Production front "After" also fits ato-de ("Afterwards, after"). should-fix.
- kaigi-o-shimasu: "Have a meeting" also fits かいぎがあります from Lesson 14. polish.
- eat-after-meeting-ato-de: the only "after the X" sentence that wants のあとで; the ten others use のあと、 and the front gives no way to choose. polish.

**Missing info**

- yoroshii-ima, yoroshii-ashita, yoroshii-place: no scene sets the polite register, so いいですか answers each Production front as well as よろしいですか does. should-fix ×3.
- kuruma-karite-doraibu-yesterday: the English says "rented", and I only know かります as "borrow". polish.

**Untaught prerequisite**

- chan-likes-steak-topic: wants ステーキはすきですか, and every すき card before it used が. No note. should-fix.
- yoroshii-place: ここでよろしいですか uses a で no card taught, next to あしたは and plain いま. should-fix.

**Wrong content (in notes)**

- yoroshii-2: says いい came in Lesson 9; it was Lesson 10. polish.
- chotto-yoroshii-desu-ka: calls Lesson 16's polite-decline ちょっと "casual" and skips the "a little bit" sense that actually applies. polish.
- chain-shite-kiku: credits the "ask" meaning to a に that belongs to でんわをして. polish.

**Other**

- itte-2: the note refers to "the one cell the book stars", a table I have never seen. polish.
- yoroshii-ima: the note says "only the time word changes", but あしたは also adds は, and neither note says why. polish.
