# Lesson 19 (chapter-19 + chapter-19-extras): learner pass

This chapter was pleasant to study. The new words are concrete (directions, delivery, office
errands), each new verb comes with its て-form and its dictionary form, and the extras unit turns
them into short requests I could answer from what I knew: taxi directions with を and まがって, deliveries
with とどけて, まで against までに. The notes earn their place most of the time; the いって note that
separates "say" from "go" and the まで/までに contrast pair are exactly what I needed. The problems
are mostly about English words that now point to two Japanese words: "documents" (しょるい or
しりょう), "package" (にもつ or パッケージ), "tell" and "take" in two まえに sentences. Beyond those, a
handful of Recognition hints say the answer, one sentence uses a construction nobody taught me
(いまから…ですか), and the extras repeat Lesson 17's overstated まえに rule in eight notes.

## Strong spots

- `iimasu-te-form` tells me いって can be "say" or "go" and gives a cue on both faces, so I never
  mixed it up with `bus-to-station` or `straight-next-intersection`.
- `made-ni`, `wait-until-five`, `return-by-five`, `made-ni-five` and `delivery-sunday-by-two` teach
  まで against までに as a pair, each with a note that names the other.
- `saki` and `temae` point at each other in their notes, and the taxi cards
  (`turn-before-department-store`, `taxi-turn-beyond-school`, `taxi-stop-before-intersection`,
  `taxi-stop-beyond-traffic-light`) drill both in real sentences.
- `houkoku-shimasu` separates the verb from Lesson 18's レポート, and `tomemasu` warns me off とります.
- The て-form cards (`te-form-magatte`, `te-form-totte`, `te-form-tomete`, `te-form-todokete`) each
  link to a Lesson 18 verb that changes the same way.
- `stop-in-front-of-bank` and `taxi-go-straight-this-road` explain the place sense of まえ and the
  route sense of を right where I needed it.
- Scenes on `tomorrow-afternoon-please`, `moufu-o-onegai-dekimasu-ka`, `taxi-to-ebisu-station` and
  `yes-i-will-relay` make short answer-shaped fronts fair.

## Weak spots

**ambiguous / unanswerable (should-fix)**

- `shorui`: "Documents" is already a meaning of Lesson 17's しりょう, with no note to separate them.
  `motte-kite-meeting` and `request-bring-meeting-documents` then gloss しりょう as "documents" too.
- `nimotsu`: "Package" is now both にもつ and Lesson 18's パッケージ; `made-ni-five`,
  `nimotsu-wo-todokeru-mae-ni-denwa` and `request-send-package-by-parcel` want にもつ for "the package".
- `douryou-ni-iu-mae-ni-denwa`: "before you tell your colleagues" fits おしえる and つたえる as well
  as いう.
- `nimotsu-wo-azukaru-mae-ni-namae`: "before we take your luggage" points to とる, not あずかる.

**missing-info (should-fix)**

- `heading-to-osaka-branch`: いまから…ですか for "are you headed to … now" is never explained.

**wrong-content (should-fix)**

- Extras unit: eight まえに notes say it "always follows the dictionary form", which
  `motte-kite-meeting` (かいぎのまえに) contradicts.

**leaks-answer (polish)**

- Recognition hints that say the answer: `made`, `wo-place-particle`, `de-means-particle`,
  `torimasu`, `tomemasu`, `kakimasu-l19`.
- Production hint "Past of おくります" on `okurimashita`.

**ambiguous (polish)**

- `kakimasu-l19` repeats Lesson 17's かきます "Write" card.
- `temae`: "Just before" with no cue invites まえ.
- `yuubin`: "Mail" invites メール.
- `yes-certainly-hotel`: the base card taught "Yes, certainly." without はい, this one wants はい.
- `chotto-matte-documents`: "I'll get the documents" does not lead to とります.
- `nimotsu-go-ji-made-azukatte`: "my bag" invites かばん, and "p.m." is not in the target.

**other (polish)**

- `magarimasu` note cites "this chapter's third grammar point", which a learner in Anki cannot see.
- `onegai-dekimasu-ka`: the English says "[you to do this]", the note says "when asking for a noun".
- `straight-next-intersection` note says this を means "through, not at", while the turn cards
  translate the same を as "at".
