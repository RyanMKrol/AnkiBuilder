# Chapter 20: Reading and Writing 6: My Favorite Restaurant (chapter-19), Learner

A comfortable chapter of 41 cards. Almost all of it is compounds built from single kanji I already
held from Lessons 4 and 6: the four directions (東, 西, 南, 北), 左 and 右, 口, 出, 分, 先, 生, 大, 学
and 外. Only three characters are new (輩, 輸, 洋), and each one comes back at once in a compound of
this unit. The new readings I could not have guessed (大人 otona, 右折 usetsu, 左右 sayū, 人口 jinkō,
出席 shusseki) are all taught on their own backs, which is what a reading card is for. Nothing on any
card is wrong as far as the deck has taught me. My two findings are both polish, and both are the
same thing: a word I already read, carded again in a slightly different spelling.

## Strong spots

- **The exits form one set.** 北口 (r-9e0b645acb8c), 南口 (r-268d6b3096cb), 東口 (r-53dcb0fe0927)
  and 西口 (r-883518371afb) each read from a direction card plus 口 and the 出口 / 入口 cards of
  Lesson 10. The kun readings on the backs (kita, minami, higashi, nishi plus guchi) are regular.
- **Left and right compounds.** 左手 (r-679cb7928d1a), 右手 (r-01a67595ac5b), 右側 (r-ed9d988c2f60,
  next to Lesson 6's 左側), 左折 (r-bf56da1bcfda) and 右折 (r-7eba1db06494) all read from parts I
  knew. 折 "Break; fold" from Lesson 12 makes the turn compounds make sense.
- **New characters come back straight away.** 輩 (r-527e0b8019a8) gives hai, then 先輩
  (r-7c6b99c21f22). 輸 (r-68c08b9800bf) gives yu, then 輸出 (r-310ecfe988f8). 洋 (r-2e750a2f58d7)
  "Ocean; Western" carries straight through 東洋 (r-913ad76b07ff) and 西洋 (r-081253ee3b6a).
- **Compass points keep the kanji order.** 南東 "Southeast" (r-c29637a429e6) and 北西 "Northwest"
  (r-45b4e8f59314) read character by character.
- **Minute counts follow the earlier pattern.** 五分 gofun (r-8090dd787fc2) and 十分 jup-pun
  (r-9dcb6ec62c8e) sit with Reading and Writing 3's 一分 ip-pun and 六分 rop-pun, with the same
  hyphenated romaji for the doubled consonant.
- **Formal register is marked.** 学ぶ "To learn; to study (formal)" (r-b43484cfb9ad) sets itself
  apart from 習う and 勉強する, so I am not left guessing which verb I have.

## Weak spots

**other**

- 入り口 (r-a1b4e92d7376): Lesson 10 already taught 入口 "Entrance", iriguchi. This card has the
  same English and the same reading with an extra り, and nothing on the back says it is one word
  written two ways.
- 大変な (r-f5746ff6acb8): Lesson 6 already taught 大変 "Tough (situation)". This is the same written
  word with な added, the same pattern as 下手な and 上手な in Reading and Writing 4.

Things I noticed and did not count as findings. 左右 (r-7d1d517da176) is glossed "Right and left"
while the characters run left then right; English says it that way round, and the back still
teaches sayū clearly. 大学生 (r-72df49d791de) repeats the kana deck's だいがくせい, but here it is the
kanji spelling being taught, which is the point of a reading deck. The single kanji 輩, 輸 and 洋 have
audio, each with one reading, the same split the deck used for 才 and 私.
