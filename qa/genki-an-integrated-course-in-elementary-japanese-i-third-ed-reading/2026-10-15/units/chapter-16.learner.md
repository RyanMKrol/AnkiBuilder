# Chapter 17: Reading and Writing 3: Daily Life (chapter-16), Learner

A short, friendly chapter: five new kanji (歳, 万, 百, 円, 千) and 34 words that are almost all
numbers, prices, clock times, ages and school years. Nearly every word is built from things I
already had: the numerals 一 to 十 from Lesson 4, 時 from Lesson 3, 分 and 年 and 生 from Lesson 4,
間 from 時間, and ねんせい from the kana chapter. So the fronts were easy to pin down, and the real work
was the sound changes, which each back spells out: 六百 rop-pyaku, 三百 sanbyaku, 八百 hap-pyaku,
三千 sanzen, 八千 has-sen, 一分 ip-pun, 六分 rop-pun, 十歳 jus-sai, 八歳 has-sai. The odd readings I
could not have guessed (四時 yoji, 九時 kuji, 時計 tokei, 二日間 futsukakan) are taught where I meet
them. I found one small gap and nothing that stops a card from being studied.

## Strong spots

- The sound-changed numbers each carry their own reading, so I never have to guess where a p, b or
  z appears: 六百 (r-29cd7f918667), 三百 (r-82566fcea2c2), 八百 (r-13c822131f65), 三千
  (r-3c929b62f1a5), 八千 (r-6dc0109470de).
- The irregular clock times are taught on the back: 四時 yoji (r-6fd47d1cbe79), 七時 shichiji
  (r-9853d6b13a8e), 九時 kuji (r-130ba6790b0a). 九時 matches 九月 ku-gatsu from Lesson 4.
- 二日間 futsukakan (r-1a15a7ab6c24) is readable because Lesson 12's 二日酔い already taught that 二日
  can be futsuka, and 間 as kan comes from 時間.
- 時計 (r-b20ce68653e0) reuses 計 from 体温計, and its irregular to is on the back.
- 万 against 一万 and 百 against 百円 give the bare kanji and a word using it, which made both stick.
