# Lesson 5: Shopping (2), learner pass

Lesson 5 was a heavy one to study but not a confusing one. Most of the base unit is drill: the ほん, まい and つ counters from one to ten, floors with かい, and a set of colours. Those cards are plain one-word cards with clear category labels, and I could answer nearly all of them from the card alone. The sentences build cleanly on Lesson 4: この/その/あの in front of nouns, どこ, and どこの for where a thing comes from. Answer cards like さんがいです and ちかいっかいです carry a scene, so I always knew what they were answering. The trouble is mostly in the notes. Several state a rule that a card I already studied contradicts, and a few talk about "the set" or "the dialogue" as if I could see the cards next to each other. A smaller group of Production fronts accept two correct answers, because this chapter teaches two ways to say the same thing and the front does not pick one.

## Strong spots

- The counter drill (chapter-5/counter-hon-_, counter-mai-_, counter-tsu-*, two-hon, two-mai) is clean. Each front names the object type, so I never had to guess between counters. The とお note (counter-tsu-10) is a good one: it warns about the と particle and about the missing つ.
- The この/その/あの cards (chapter-5/kono, sono, ano) explain the difference from これ/それ/あれ clearly and briefly.
- Answer cards with scenes: chapter-5/fib-c5-vacuum-third-floor, fib-c5-pc-basement-first-floor, chapter-5-extras/iie-rokkai-desu, iie-suisu-no-ja-arimasen, fib-kyuukai-desu, ja-sore-o-nihon-kudasai. Each scene tells me what is being answered without giving the answer away.
- chapter-5-extras/1st-floor has the one note in the chapter I would have missed most without it (いっかい is the British ground floor).
- The colour-noun cards (chapter-5-extras/green, purple, pink, orange, grey) put の in the target, which makes the noun/adjective split something I practise, not just read about.

## Weak spots

**Notes that teach something false, judged against earlier cards (should-fix)**

- chapter-5-extras/hai-sore-mo-nihon-no: the note says the camera is now "the near one" for the assistant, which by this lesson's own rule means これ, not the それ in the target.
- chapter-5-extras/kore-wa-igirisu-no-kaban: "a place before の ... never who it belongs to" is contradicted by かいしゃのでんわ, のぞみデパートのでんわばんごう and とうきょうだいがくのラジャ.
- chapter-5-extras/akawain-o-ippon-kudasai: the ほん to ぽん list leaves out はち, though chapter-5/counter-hon-8 teaches はっぽん.
- chapter-5-extras/koppu-futatsu-to-osara-sanmai: "glasses are round so they take つ" conflicts with the base note that つ is the general counter.
- chapter-5-extras/rooftop: links おくじょう to ちゅうしゃじょう, a word I have never met, through a "place" meaning that seems doubtful for "rooftop".

**Production fronts with more than one right answer**

- chapter-5/doumo (should-fix): "Thanks." versus two cards I know as "Thank you."
- chapter-5-extras/irl-l1-29 (should-fix): "Where are you from?" is what I answer with おくにはどちらですか.
- chapter-5-extras/eg-kiiro-no-kutsushita (should-fix) and eg-kiiroi-kasa (polish): the yellow note says both forms are correct, so neither front can ask for the form its card teaches.
- chapter-5-extras/sono-wain-doko-no and ano-kamera-wa-doko-no (polish): "Where is that X from?" has two shapes in this chapter, and other cards with the same English use the other shape.
- chapter-5/fib-c5-coffee-cup-origin-a and chapter-5-extras/furansu-no-desu (polish): "It's from England/France" fits a bare Xです, which every earlier "from X" card used.
- chapter-5-extras/5th-floor and 1st-floor (polish): duplicates of chapter-5/fifth-floor and ikkai-sub.

**Notes that point at things I cannot see (polish)**

- chapter-5-extras/sore-wa-itaria-no-kutsu-ka ("Member 3 of the set", "これ becomes それ") and fukuro-mo-ni-mai-kudasai ("two lines earlier in the same dialogue").
- chapter-5/achira defines あちら through あそこ, which no card has taught by this point.
- chapter-5-extras/dore-desu-ka sends me to Lesson 1 for どちら "which of two", but Lesson 1 taught どちら as polite "where".
- chapter-5-extras/irl-l1-29 says the ジャック sentence is "in this lesson"; it is in Lesson 1's extras.

**Small wording slips (polish)**

- chapter-5/fib-c5-coffee-cup-quantity: "Then" in the English with no じゃ in the target.
- chapter-5-extras/irl-l1-40: "Where is the hotel address?" is not natural English.
- chapter-5/cheesecake: "Cheese cake" as two words.
- chapter-5/counter-hon-8: romaji "happon" where the rest of the series is split ("ip-pon", "rop-pon").
