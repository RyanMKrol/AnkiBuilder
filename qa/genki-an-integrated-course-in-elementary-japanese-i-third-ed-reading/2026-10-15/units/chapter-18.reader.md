## Findings

**book-gap**

- `-` (様 / 〜さま): the chapter glosses 〜さま "Mr./Ms. (used in letter writing)" under the postcard,
  and 様 "Mr./Ms." in the Japanese addresses note. No card in the collection has either form. The
  build's coverage adversary listed 様 as its one gap (`candidates/coverage.json`), but it never
  reached the corpus. Under rule 2 the card is the kanji 様, read さま. should-fix.
- chapter-17 `r-482dccb7367c` (三日): this chapter teaches 三日 as みっか, the 3rd of the month, in the
  same series as the ten date cards it ships. The card from RW4 carries only "Three days". The build
  dropped this chapter's 三日 as already carded, so the date sense never reached the one card that
  has this written form. A learner sees 二日 "The 2nd (day of the month)" next to 三日 "Three days".
  should-fix.
