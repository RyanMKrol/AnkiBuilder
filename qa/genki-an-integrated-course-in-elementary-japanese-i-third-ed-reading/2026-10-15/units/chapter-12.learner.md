# Chapter 13: Lesson 11: After the Vacation (chapter-12), Learner

A big chapter, 60 new single kanji and 56 words, with three clusters: New Year (大晦日, 年越しそば,
初詣, お年玉, 年賀状, お節料理, 雑煮, よいお年を), jobs (作家, 教師, 歌手, 俳優, 看護師, 消防士,
警察官, 宇宙飛行士, 漫画家, 研究者) and classroom words (授業, 文法, 発音, 質問, 意味, 行目, 開く,
閉じる). It was easy to study. Almost every word is built from a kanji card I met a few cards
earlier or in Lesson 10 (宇 and 宙 then 飛行機's 飛 and 行 for 宇宙飛行士; 消 and 防 plus the new 士
for 消防士; 留 plus 学 for 留学する), and the readings I could not have worked out (雑煮 zōni with
the new zō for 雑, 大晦日 ōmisoka, 数の子 kazunoko, 行目 gyōme) are each given on the back. Fronts
never collide: the near pairs differ in their written form and the English keeps them apart. I
found one small thing, and nothing that stops a card from being studied.

## Strong spots

- Verb pairs from earlier lessons stay distinct: 開く "To open (a book)" against Lesson 6's 開ける
  "To open (something)" (r-119daf93eeb9), 閉じる "To close (a book)" against 閉める
  (r-3da5c1855151), 取る "To take (a class); to get (a grade)" against Lesson 4's 撮る "To take (a
  picture)" (r-244b0ce365f3), and 飼う "To own (a pet)" against 買う, with 飼 "Keep (an animal);
  raise" to anchor it (r-440682eae91b, r-13a04753b828).
- The kanji cards for the job suffixes make the job words readable before turning over: 士
  "Samurai; professional" (r-3955785dea35), 師 "Teacher; master" (r-b48e9d30fb4e), 官 "Official"
  (r-966c12621f7d), with Lesson 10's 者 "Person (who does something)" for 研究者.
- 練習 "Practice; exercise (drill)" (r-2ff4a6f70b6b) sits fine beside Lesson 10's 練習する "To
  practice", and 意味 "Meaning" (r-2353efbc3e76) confirms the "Meaning" half of 味 "Meaning; taste".
- 遅刻する "To be late (for an appointment)" (r-0412545cefa6) says what kind of late, which keeps it
  apart from Lesson 8's 遅い "Slow; late".

## Weak spots

**other (1, polish)**

- 宇宙飛行士 (r-8baf85e00cb0) prints its romaji as one unbroken "uchūhikōshi", while the other
  two-word compounds in the deck are split (天気予報 "tenki yohō", 短期大学 "tanki daigaku", スポーツ選手
  "supōtsu senshu" in this chapter). It is the longest romaji in the unit and the hardest to sound
  out in one piece.

Nothing from preflight to set aside: the unit's only preflight lines are the two exclusion notes,
and the excluded phrases are not shown to the learner.
